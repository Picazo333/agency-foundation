# Visual Taste Summary

Core tension: **the coexistence of sacred beauty and horror**.

High-signal ingredients:
- colorful medieval illuminated manuscripts / codices
- angels, bestiaries, hybrid creatures and symbolic grotesque
- structural darkness, chiaroscuro and nocturnal sacred atmosphere
- classical anatomical / medical engravings
- expressionist spatial distortion and black-and-white cinema
- ivory/parchment + black/charcoal + ultramarine + crimson/vermilion + aged gold
- editorial typography, annotation and information-rich systems

Anti-profile:
- purple/cyan AI gradients
- neon tech
- glassmorphism
- generic SaaS bento
- pseudo-luxury black/gold
- canned dark academia / occult shortcuts
- trendy brutalism / random grunge
- fantasy-medieval cosplay
- chaos without hierarchy

Current high-signal directions: Illuminated Chirograph, Sacred Anatomy, Angelic Grotesque, Cathedral of Systems, Anatomical Bestiary.
