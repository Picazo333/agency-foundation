# BOLD Contrarian Summary

BOLD is retained primarily as a strategic falsification input. Its strongest implication is that a generic horizontal AI/automation agency may be commercially weak/commoditized, and that a more diagnostic-led, productized or Commercial-Ops/RevOps-oriented model may be stronger.

This is **not** a canonical business decision. It must be confronted against Spark research, founder capabilities, ICP access, real buyer evidence, pricing, proofability, delivery constraints and field validation.

Use it to ask harder questions, not to force a predetermined agency form.
