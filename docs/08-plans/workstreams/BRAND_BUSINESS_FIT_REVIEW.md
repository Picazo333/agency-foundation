# Brand / Business Fit Review

Required inputs: Brand Direction V0 + Agency Master Plan.

Review questions:
- Does the name still fit the category/ICP/offer?
- Does the visual identity support the intended price/trust level?
- Does it over-constrain the agency to AI, healthcare or another narrow category?
- Does it still work if the business becomes productized, consultancy-led or creative-systems-led?
- Does it communicate the right balance of creativity, rigor, systems and implementation capability?
- Are any changes structural enough to require mutation or kill of Brand V0?

Outcome: PASS / MUTATE / KILL, with ADR if canonical direction changes.
