# Naming Workbench

## Current state
**DIVINIVID is the working selected Brand name for identity-system exploration.**

Do not reopen broad naming exploration during the frozen visual-identity plan unless:
- final legal/trademark/domain diligence surfaces a material blocker;
- a serious linguistic/reputational issue is discovered; or
- the human owner explicitly reopens naming.

Working selection is not trademark clearance or Brand V1 promotion.

## Evaluation dimensions retained
- semantic strength;
- pronunciation ES/EN;
- memorability;
- spelling friction;
- distinctiveness;
- extensibility beyond AI/marketing/healthcare;
- category collisions;
- searchability / preliminary ownability signals;
- wordmark potential;
- narrative potential.

## Naming exploration progression

### Early research territories
Historical starting candidates included Chirograph, Horologium, Macario, Ossein, Umbraculum, Canonicus, Lucernaria, Basalt, Vertebra and Apocrypha. These are exploration history, not active candidates.

### Founder phonetic/semantic anchors
`Marrow` became an important benchmark for sound, inner structure, substance and imagery, while preliminary screening exposed an adjacent design/brand collision risk.

Other attractive source words included `Nacre`, `Rood`, `Psalm`, `Vow`, `Hymn`, `Mana` and related sacred/material/phonetic families.

### Palindrome / invented-word exploration
The process then shifted deliberately toward coined palindromes and phonetic mutation. Materially explored candidates included:
- Manam / Maanaam family;
- Reviver;
- Deified;
- Animina;
- Divinivid;
- other short invented palindrome experiments that were later discarded.

### Strong late-stage finalists
The last meaningful shortlist before the current selection centered on:
- **DIVINIVID** — invented palindrome; semantic resonance around divine / vivid / living divinity; unusually strong structural potential for identity exploration;
- **MAANAAM** — invented palindrome from the Mana/Manam phonetic family; ritual/mantra/symmetry qualities, with greater nearby-name/search collision risk in preliminary exploration;
- other earlier finalists were downgraded after collision, semantic or fit concerns.

## Current selection rationale
DIVINIVID advances because the human owner explicitly selected it for continued identity exploration and because its structure supports the Brand's current thesis without dictating one literal historical style.

The palindrome is a **design opportunity, not a logo requirement**. Symmetry must prove useful rather than become a gimmick.

Before final Brand V1 or commercial launch, run appropriate current diligence for:
- trademark/category conflict;

- domain/handle strategy;
- linguistic/pronunciation concerns;
- confusingly similar commercial names.

Do not represent preliminary web screening as legal clearance.
