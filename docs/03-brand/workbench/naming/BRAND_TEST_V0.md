# Brand Test V0 — DIVINIVID vs MAANAAM

## Purpose
Test whether the two strongest naming candidates can carry a serious, ownable visual identity before any naming freeze.

This is **not** a final identity system and **not** a moodboard exercise. Both candidates must be tested under comparable conditions.

## Shared aesthetic constraints
- sacred beauty + symbolic horror / uncanny tension;
- illuminated-manuscript intelligence without medieval cosplay;
- anatomy / body / sacred matter as optional image system;
- ivory, deep black, aged gold, ultramarine and restrained crimson as the initial test palette;
- no cyan, neon, AI-purple, glassmorphism, generic SaaS gradients or gothic cliché;
- contemporary editorial precision;
- web-capable, system-capable, premium but not pseudo-luxury.

## Candidate A — DIVINIVID
### Verbal thesis
Invented palindrome carrying resonances of divine / vivid / living divinity / transfiguration.

### Visual hypotheses to test
- mirrored wordmark around the central `N`;
- sacred geometry without literal religious logo tropes;
- vertical / axial symmetry;
- sharper, more transcendent, icon-like behavior;
- stronger contrast between luminous and dark states;
- ability to support both editorial and software/UI contexts.

## Candidate B — MAANAAM
### Verbal thesis
Invented palindrome derived from the Mana/Manam sound family; mantra, essence, ritual, return, reflected energy.

### Visual hypotheses to test
- mirrored wordmark around the central `N`;
- softer, more organic and material symmetry;
- manuscript / ritual / sacred-object behavior;
- possibility of a seal-like mark without falling into occult branding;
- stronger relationship to body, matter, nacre, vellum and sacred substance;
- ability to remain contemporary in product/UI contexts.

## Controlled test surfaces
Each candidate must be shown in the same seven contexts:
1. primary wordmark;
2. mirrored / reduced mark exploration;
3. typography pairing;
4. palette behavior;
5. editorial cover or manifesto page;
6. website hero / interface fragment;
7. motion concept described as a short sequence.

## Evaluation dimensions
- immediate memorability;
- visual distinctiveness;
- readability;
- pronunciation/spelling friction;
- symbolic depth;
- ability to express sacred beauty + darkness;
- contemporary credibility;
- B2B credibility;
- extensibility beyond one service model;
- UI/web fitness;
- ability to own symmetry rather than use it as decoration;
- risk of looking fantasy, occult, fashion, fragrance or generic luxury;
- risk of becoming visually dependent on historical imagery.

## Pass criteria
A candidate passes Brand Test V0 only if:
- it works in plain black type before decoration;
- the palindrome provides useful structure rather than gimmick;
- it can survive a clean interface without medieval imagery;
- it can also support the richer sacred/anatomical illustration system;
- it feels credible on a proposal, contract, website, software surface and case study;
- it produces at least one distinctive wordmark direction without harming legibility.

## Decision policy
Do not score a winner before both candidates are visualized under identical conditions.
Do not freeze naming based only on the most decorative execution.
If both pass, retain both until Brand/Business Fit Review or until one clearly dominates in controlled visual testing.
