# Brand Test V0 — Historical DIVINIVID vs MAANAAM

> **Historical artifact.** This controlled test was defined before the human owner selected DIVINIVID for continued identity exploration. It is retained for rationale/traceability and does not reopen the naming gate.

## Original purpose
Test whether the two strongest candidates could carry a serious visual identity under comparable conditions before any final naming freeze.

## Shared aesthetic constraints used at that time
- sacred beauty + symbolic horror / uncanny tension;
- illuminated-manuscript intelligence without medieval cosplay;
- anatomy / body / sacred matter as optional image system;
- ivory, deep black, aged gold, ultramarine and restrained crimson as initial test territory;
- no cyan, neon, AI-purple, glassmorphism, generic SaaS gradients or gothic cliché;
- contemporary editorial precision;
- web-capable, system-capable, premium but not pseudo-luxury.

## Candidate A — DIVINIVID
### Verbal thesis
Invented palindrome carrying resonances of divine / vivid / living divinity / transfiguration.

### Visual hypotheses
- mirrored or axial behavior around the word structure;
- sacred geometry without literal religious-logo tropes;
- vertical/bilateral symmetry as an optional structural tool;
- sharper/transcendent/icon-like behavior;
- strong contrast between luminous and dark states;
- ability to support editorial and software/UI contexts.

## Candidate B — MAANAAM
### Verbal thesis
Invented palindrome from the Mana/Manam phonetic family; mantra, essence, ritual, return, reflected energy.

### Visual hypotheses
- softer/material/organic symmetry;
- manuscript/ritual/sacred-object behavior;
- possible seal logic without occult branding;
- relationship to body, nacre, vellum and sacred matter;
- need to remain contemporary in product/UI contexts.

## Controlled test surfaces
1. primary wordmark;
2. mirrored/reduced mark exploration;
3. typography pairing;
4. palette behavior;
5. editorial cover/manifesto page;
6. website hero/interface fragment;
7. motion concept.

## Evaluation dimensions
- memorability;
- visual distinctiveness;
- readability;
- pronunciation/spelling friction;
- symbolic depth;
- sacred beauty + darkness capability;
- contemporary credibility;
- B2B credibility;
- extensibility;
- UI/web fitness;
- useful versus gimmicky symmetry;
- risk of fantasy/occult/fashion/fragrance/generic luxury;
- dependency on historical imagery.

## Original pass criteria
A candidate needed to work in plain black type before decoration, make palindrome structure useful rather than gimmicky, survive a clean interface, support richer sacred/anatomical imagery and remain credible across proposal, contract, web, software and case-study contexts.

## Current state
The human owner subsequently selected **DIVINIVID** for continued identity exploration. The frozen visual-identity plan therefore no longer requires a DIVINIVID-vs-MAANAAM visual contest. Final legal/trademark/domain diligence remains a separate later requirement.
