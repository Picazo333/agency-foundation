# Naming Long List V1

## Purpose
First curated naming longlist after internal filtering for cliché, obvious generic AI/agency language, poor ES/EN pronunciation, weak extensibility, and obvious direct category collisions discovered in a quick web screen.

This is **not** trademark clearance, domain clearance, or final availability validation.

## Founder signal: MARROW
`Marrow` remains an important phonetic and semantic benchmark because the sound and imagery strongly resonate with the desired brand direction: inner structure, substance, depth, core matter.

However, a quick current web screen found an existing `Marrow` brand identity / strategic design studio. Therefore `Marrow` should currently be treated as a **reference anchor, not a clean final candidate**, unless a later legal/category assessment reveals an acceptable path.

## Names removed before presentation because of obvious direct/near-direct category collisions
Examples include: Ductus, Ashlar, Limen, Tessera, Spolia, Siglum, Ramus, Pith, Nidus, Caisson, Archivolt, Silex, Quoin, Parvis, Murex, Brume, Scholium, Narthex, Armarium, Indicia, Intrados, Diopter, Morven.

The point of this first filter is not legal certainty; it is to avoid wasting creative attention on names already visibly occupied in adjacent creative/technology/agency categories.

# Territory 01 — Script / Trace / Inscription

### 1. Sigla
Compact, manuscript-adjacent, systematic. **Caution:** quick screen shows multiple communication/architecture uses; likely high collision risk despite semantic fit.

### 2. Notae
Evokes notes, marks, notation and recorded knowledge. Soft but not delicate; potentially strong editorial behavior.

### 3. Quire
A gathering of folded sheets in bookmaking. Short, tactile, historical, system-like. Requires collision and pronunciation screening.

### 4. Colophon
A publishing/end-matter term associated with authorship and production. Strong narrative but longer and more explicitly editorial.

### 5. Codicil
A formal addition/amendment to an existing document. Interesting for systems that evolve without discarding prior structure; legal association must be tested.

---

# Territory 02 — Anatomy / Structure / Inner Form

### 6. Marrow — REFERENCE ANCHOR
Excellent founder fit, but visible direct collision with an existing design/brand studio means it is not currently a clean candidate.

### 7. Ossein
Organic material within bone. Strong imagery: structure without superficiality, anatomical intelligence, inner matter. One of the strongest current candidates.

### 8. Trabecula
Structural strut / lattice within tissue and bone. Rich systems metaphor; longer and somewhat technical, so pronunciation friction must be tested.

### 9. Plica
Fold or ridge in anatomy. Short, visual and structural. Potentially elegant wordmark; requires semantic/collision screening.

### 10. Raphe
A seam or line of union in anatomy. Strong conceptual fit with integration and connection; pronunciation in ES/EN is a material risk.

---

# Territory 03 — Architecture / Order / Construction

### 11. Extrados
Outer surface of an arch or vault. Architectural, structural, uncommon, and visually strong. Slightly technical but memorable.

### 12. Tracery
Ornamental structural stonework in Gothic architecture. Excellent bridge between system and ornament; could lean too decorative if not controlled.

### 13. Spandrel
Architectural space created by structural geometry; also has systems/evolutionary associations. Strong intellectual narrative, somewhat long.

### 14. Aedicule
A small architectural shrine/niche. High visual potential; sacred/architectural resonance, but pronunciation and literal religious association require testing.

### 15. Squinch
Architectural device that transitions a square space into a dome-supporting form. Great transformation metaphor; phonetics are unusual and may feel too quirky.

---

# Territory 04 — Observation / Instrument / Measure

### 16. Ephemeris
A table of calculated celestial positions over time. Rich in observation, prediction, systems and astronomy. Long but distinctive.

### 17. Alidade
A sighting device used in surveying/navigation. Strong metaphor for orientation and precise observation; relatively uncommon.

### 18. Abscissa
Coordinate/measurement term. Intellectual, graphic, structured. Needs ES/EN pronunciation and gender/word-feel testing.

### 19. Isogon
A line connecting equal values of an angular quantity, especially magnetic declination. Compact, technical, ownable-feeling, potentially too scientific.

### 20. Ecliptic
The apparent path of the Sun across the celestial sphere. Strong celestial imagery and visual potential; likely higher collision than rarer terms.

---

# Territory 05 — Archive / Memory / Knowledge

### 21. Variorum
An edition containing notes/readings from multiple sources. Excellent metaphor for synthesis, comparison and accumulated intelligence. Strong candidate.

### 22. Tabularium
The Roman state archive. Monumental, archival, institutional. Long and more historical, but potentially powerful.

### 23. Florilegium
A collection of selected passages/excerpts; literally a gathering of flowers. Beautiful concept of curation and knowledge harvest; long and literary.

### 24. Excerpta
Collected extracts or selections. Editorial, compact enough, and useful as a synthesis metaphor. Needs exact linguistic/brand screening.

### 25. Cartulary
A collection/register of charters or records. Strong archive/system concept; pronunciation and legal-historical tone need testing.

---

# Territory 06 — Liminal / Sacred / Threshold

### 26. Votive
Something offered or dedicated in fulfillment of a vow. Sacred without being explicitly denominational; elegant and visually fertile. Requires brand collision screen.

### 27. Chancel
Architectural sacred-space term. Strong structure/threshold resonance, but church association is explicit and may constrain interpretation.

### 28. Lustrum
A period/cycle and historically a purification rite. Excellent sound, compactness and concept of renewal; needs semantic screening across languages.

### 29. Oriel
An architectural projecting window; light, threshold and observation associations. Short and elegant, but more architectural than systems-oriented.

### 30. Vespertine
Of the evening; nocturnal without using obvious gothic language. Strong atmosphere, but long and potentially literary/fashion-coded.

---

# Territory 07 — Matter / Relic / Object

### 31. Porphyry
Historic purple-red stone associated with permanence, materiality and imperial craft. Visually rich, unusual, somewhat difficult to spell.

### 32. Ferrule
A ring/sleeve that strengthens or joins an object. Excellent systems metaphor: reinforcement and connection. Industrial but refined.

### 33. Sinter
Material formed by heat/pressure binding particles without fully melting them. Strong transformation/process metaphor; technical-industrial tone.

### 34. Intaglio
Incised/engraved image-making process. Directly aligned with engraving and material craft; could read as art/print studio if too literal.

### 35. Nacre
Mother-of-pearl material. Layering, iridescence, organic structure and refinement. Softer than the rest; potential fashion/luxury associations.

---

# Territory 08 — Opaque / Proprietary Phonetic Experiments

These are deliberately less semantic. The goal is to test whether the brand can carry meaning rather than inherit it. They require the strictest collision and language screening.

### 36. Vellor
Heavy but smooth phonetics; faint echoes of vellum without literal dependence.

### 37. Mavren
Dark, compact and memorable; intentionally opaque. Must be checked for fantasy/name associations.

### 38. Verren
Firm, neutral, institutional sound. Low semantic baggage by design.

### 39. Serren
Controlled, slightly editorial sound; could support a strong wordmark. Must avoid generic startup-name feel.

### 40. Orren
Simple and pronounceable; comparatively neutral. Requires screening for surname/place/company collisions.

# Current signal leaders — not a ranking

The names with the strongest conceptual signal for the next round are currently:

- `Ossein` — strongest anatomy/structure alternative to the Marrow idea;
- `Variorum` — strongest knowledge/synthesis concept;
- `Alidade` — strong observation/orientation instrument metaphor;
- `Ferrule` — strong systems/connection/material metaphor;
- `Lustrum` — compact, ritual/cycle/renewal territory;
- `Tracery` — structure + ornament bridge;
- `Votive` — sacred tension without directly naming angels/demons;
- `Quire` — manuscript/craft/system route.

`Marrow` remains the founder-preference benchmark even though direct category collision makes it a poor clean candidate at this stage.

## Next step

Founder reaction first. Then reduce to roughly 12–18 names and run a materially deeper screen for:

- direct commercial collisions;
- pronunciation/spelling ES/EN;
- semantic baggage;
- domain/search feasibility;
- visual/wordmark behavior;
- extensibility;
- preliminary trademark risk.

Do not develop full identities for this entire longlist.
