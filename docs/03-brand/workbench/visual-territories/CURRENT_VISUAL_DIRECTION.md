# Current Visual Direction

## Current hierarchy
- Brand: **DIVINIVID**
- Route 01 internal codename: **KIROGRAF**
- Route 01 descriptor: **Sacred Anatomy**

## Core thesis
> **Contrast with elegance the tension and harmony between the divine and the grotesque, darkness and light, showing how apparent opposites can coexist inside one system.**

## Five master tensions
1. Divine ↔ Grotesque
2. Light ↔ Darkness
3. Order ↔ Aberration
4. Knowledge ↔ Mystery
5. Body ↔ Transcendence

## Strong source territories
- illuminated manuscripts/codices with real polychromy;
- angels, bestiaries, hybrids and historical sacred/grotesque imagery;
- chiaroscuro/tenebrism and large meaningful darkness;
- historical anatomical engraving and scientific plate logic;
- expressionist distortion / Caligari-like spatial tension;
- black-and-white cinema and nocturnal sacred atmosphere;
- cosmological/historical diagrammatics;
- material sources such as paper, vellum, ink, engraving, pigment, gold leaf, stone and aged metal;
- editorial/information structure as a container rather than the aesthetic thesis.

## High-signal color territory
Ink/charcoal and ivory/parchment form a base. Ultramarine, carmine/vermilion and aged gold are high-signal historical pigment territories. Final palette values are not frozen.
