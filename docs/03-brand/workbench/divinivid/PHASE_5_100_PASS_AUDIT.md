# DIVINIVID — Phase 5 Corrected Plan: 100-Pass Audit

## Purpose
Stress-test the corrected anchored-derivation plan through 100 explicit review passes before using it as the next visual-production method.

This is not 100 aesthetic rewrites. It is a structured matrix of 10 audit lenses × 10 passes each. The goal is to find process defects before spending more visual-generation cycles.

---

# Audit matrix

Checked:
1. whether early human-approved work remains the primary positive evidence;
2. whether newer work can accidentally outrank it;
3. whether exact images must be pinned rather than reconstructed from memory;
4. whether principles are separated from literal pixels;
5. whether three anchors can remain distinct;
6. whether route labels bias evaluation;
7. whether artifact-specific composition is over-preserved;

PASS after adding the hard `A0` recovery gate and seven-layer DNA decomposition.

# Corrections produced by the 100-pass audit

The audit yielded the following material corrections compared with the prior reconciliation:

1. **Primary positive evidence reset.** The first serious human-approved exploration set outranks BS-04 and all later probes.
2. **Exact anchor recovery gate added.** Do not approximate the best three images from memory.
3. **Specimen-first correction.** Organism morphology is one layer, not the identity objective.
4. **Seven-layer DNA decomposition added.** Image, composition, revelation, liturgy, typography behavior, material/color and information are separated.
5. **Comparable shell added.** Every candidate carries equivalent evidence.
6. **Six-child ceiling added.** The first derivation round is deliberately bounded.
7. **Two contrasts per anchor max.** Prevents brute-force combinatorial drift.
8. **Editable assembly precedes generation when possible.** Not every design question needs new pixels.
9. **Brand Without Beast made mandatory.** Directly addresses over-focus on the specimen.
10. **Beast Without Brand added.** Correctly classifies beautiful but generic imagery as asset language.
11. **Liturgical influence promoted from vague score to controlled lane.**
12. **Dark Revelation promoted from “make darker” to semantic-revelation behavior.**
13. **Typography frozen to roles only.** No final font decision in Phase 5.
14. **Applications remain downstream.** No web/site/mockup collage during derivative selection.
15. **Per-layer feedback replaces whole-board preference as primary diagnostic.**
16. **Versioned funnel added.** 3 anchors → 6 children → 3 survivors → 2 proof directions → 1 pass/convergence.
17. **External workflow search completed.** Useful patterns incorporated without adding unnecessary dependency or conflicting authority.
18. **Phase-5 exit explicitly reconnects to the frozen master plan.**

# Final audit verdict

`PASS WITH TWO EXECUTION BLOCKERS`

The corrected process is strong enough to govern the next visual work, but production must not resume until:

1. the exact three Golden Anchor artifacts are recovered/pinned;
2. `BRAND_STATE.md` and `phase5_iteration_state.yaml` are updated so a new agent will not restart from BS-04 / ORG-PROBE-01.

Once those two blockers close, proceed to Anchor DNA extraction — **not image generation**.
