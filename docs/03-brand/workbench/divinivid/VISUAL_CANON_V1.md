## Canon interpretation

### Living Darkness
Black is not a neutral background. It behaves as spatial/material absence: absorbs, conceals, reveals, compresses and controls emphasis.

### Living Crimson
Crimson is not a decorative accent. It represents matter, signal, marrow, fracture, transformation, active state or rupture.

### Ivory
Ivory is the principal editorial/light counterform: legibility, specimen field, sacred illumination and controlled contrast.

### Aged gold
Gold is primarily instrument, hierarchy, annotation, measurement and liturgical order.

### Ultramar
Ultramar has selective permission. It can become materially stronger in medieval/bestiary/celestial systems but should not become an omnipresent Brand accent.

### Anatomy by analogy
Anatomy is translated through structural analogy rather than literal gore:
- mountain ↔ fractured bone;
- river ↔ marrow;
- cell ↔ celestial body;
- chamber ↔ organ/cavity;
- vessel ↔ specimen/world container.

### Sacred beauty + disturbance
The dominant tension remains:
> beauty / order / sacredness ↔ anomaly / horror / impossible life

The result must not collapse into generic gothic, occult, dark-academia or fantasy imagery.

### Bestiary
Entity systems draw from medieval/codex logic, celestial hierarchy, liminal grotesque, infernal families and abstract organisms. They are symbolic systems, not videogame concept art.

### Environments
Architecture, chambers, landscapes and impossible geometry may behave as organisms or cognitive/spiritual structures.

### A/B/H intensity
- **A — Dramatic / Revelatory:** richer imagery, larger transformation, stronger crimson/material events.
- **B — Abyssal / Subtle:** black dominates; imagery becomes sparse, elegant and low-amplitude.
- **H — Hybrid:** system chooses intensity by context and hierarchy.

### Motion
Motion explains behavior and transformation. It must not become ambient spectacle or generic kinetic design.

### Information layer
Typography, annotation, measurement and data are surgical, editorial and disciplined. This information order is the counterweight to the dreamlike visual universe.
