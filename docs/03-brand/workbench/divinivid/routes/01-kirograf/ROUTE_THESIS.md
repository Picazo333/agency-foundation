# Route 01 — KIROGRAF / Sacred Anatomy

## Hierarchy
`DIVINIVID` is the Brand. `KIROGRAF` is an internal route codename. `Sacred Anatomy` is its descriptor.

Never place KIROGRAF where a customer-facing application should say DIVINIVID.

## Route thesis
> **Anatomical and classificatory knowledge forms the rational skeleton; sacred illumination, symbolic grotesque and selective darkness reveal that the body, the anomaly and the transcendent belong to the same system.**

## Primary tensions emphasized
1. Knowledge ↔ Mystery
2. Body ↔ Transcendence
3. Divine ↔ Grotesque
4. Light ↔ Darkness
5. Order ↔ Aberration

## Structural ingredients
- historical anatomical engraving / section / plate;
- labels, axes, folios, annotation, measurement and classification;
- illuminated-manuscript pigment and sacred narrative intervention;
- historical grotesque/bestiary/marginal anomaly;
- tenebrist semantic light;
- contemporary editorial/digital discipline as container rather than aesthetic replacement.

## Not KIROGRAF
- a medical brand;
- a gore identity;
- a black/gold luxury identity;
- an occult/tarot identity;
- a collection of medieval illustrations;
- a generic editorial serif system;
- a Swiss-grid identity;
- a statue-based “sacred” moodboard;
- a fantasy game universe.

## Phase-5 5-Up hypotheses
A. Illumination Invades Anatomy
B. Grotesque Inside Order
C. Light Reveals the Uncomfortable
D. Sacred Marginalia
E. Celestial / Corporeal Mirror

These are hypotheses inside KIROGRAF, not separate brands.

## Route success condition
KIROGRAF succeeds only if:
- anatomy has semantic function;
- sacred and grotesque can coexist without shock gimmicks;
- the system supports both rich historical imagery and imagery-free states;
- the signature reads as DIVINIVID without relying on the route codename;
- digital/product surfaces remain usable and serious;
- the result cannot be reduced to the Anti-Canon defaults.

## Current state
Conceptual route thesis is approved as a hypothesis. Visual execution is **not approved**. Previous generated rounds are not the Golden Benchmark and must not be treated as route canon.
