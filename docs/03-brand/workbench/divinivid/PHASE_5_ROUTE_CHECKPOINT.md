# DIVINIVID — Phase 5 Five-Route Visual Checkpoint

## Checkpoint declaration
The visual-exploration reset has reached a stable checkpoint.

Five independent visual territories have now been explored and explicitly human-approved. Each route has two complementary boards:

1. **Moodboard I — Identity / Atmosphere**
   - establishes thesis, emotional register, image language, composition, provisional typographic voice, color/material cues and primary symbolic behavior;
2. **Moodboard II — System / Surface**
   - demonstrates how the route behaves as a design system through material/texture, typography in use, web/interface fragments, composition grammar, graphic micro-language, image-treatment rules, light/dark behavior, motion vocabulary, reduced state and anti-patterns.

### Frozen rule
A route is not considered sufficiently evidenced for convergence unless **both Moodboard I and Moodboard II are approved**.

This two-board rule is now part of the Phase-5 process.

# Approved Route Set

## R1 — Dark Revelation
**Status:** APPROVED — 2/2 boards.

### Core proposition
Meaning is created through concealment, semantic darkness and selective revelation.

### Distinctive mechanisms
- large negative space;
- veil / threshold / apparition;
- tenebrist light used semantically rather than decoratively;
- restrained crimson and aged gold;
- quiet ceremonial framing;
- identity capable of surviving with little or no hero imagery.

### Hard anti-leak
- no specimen taxonomy;
- no scientific dossier dominance;
- no anatomy-as-spectacle;
- no dark luxury / occult branding / neon dark-tech.

---

## R2 — Bestiary of Systems
**Status:** APPROVED — 2/2 boards.

### Core proposition
Impossible living forms can be observed, classified and contained without being fully explained.

### Distinctive mechanisms
- non-human impossible organic matter;
- specimen / archive / observation logic;
- annotation and classification;
- membrane / filament / bone-like / seed-like material behavior;
- high editorial-system potential;
- multiple information densities and systematic image treatment.

### Hard anti-leak
- no dragon/wyvern/fantasy default;
- no hero-human devotional framing;
- no gothic/occult dominance;
- no sci-fi UI / cyan / neon.

---

## R3 — Sacred Corporeal
**Status:** APPROVED — 2/2 boards.

### Core proposition
The intact human body acts as a threshold between matter and transcendence.

### Distinctive mechanisms
- human figure / skin / gesture / hands;
- axial and halo-like composition;
- veil, marble, paper, cloth and sacred materiality;
- crimson as semantic interiority without exposed anatomy;
- liturgical seriousness without explicit religious branding;
- elegant light/dark continuity.

### Safety/process clarification
The route must remain **non-traumatic and non-graphic**. Explicit dissection, exposed tissue, open anatomy or literal Vesalian corporeal treatment is not required to preserve the route's thesis and previously caused avoidable generation failures.

### Hard anti-leak
- no specimen taxonomy as primary structure;
- no creature mascot;
- no explicit anatomy/gore;
- no generic wellness spirituality.

---

## R4 — Oneiric Anatomy
**Status:** APPROVED — 2/2 boards.

### Core proposition
The body is a landscape of meaning governed by dream logic rather than literal anatomy.

### Distinctive mechanisms
- marked surrealism;
- body / architecture / landscape / symbol overlap;
- impossible but non-traumatic interiority;
- collage and layered archival logic;
- veil, flora, stone, manuscript, celestial and symbolic matter;
- strong dark/light adaptability;
- high image-language fertility.

### Human signal
**Strongest explicit current inclination.** The owner described this route as especially powerful after both boards were approved.

This is a preference signal, not yet a final Brand Direction selection.

### Hard anti-leak
- no literal dissection;
- no repeated “head with cathedral” formula;
- no specimen dossier dominance;
- no stock-surreal / generic occult collage.

---

## R5 — Illuminated Marginalia
**Status:** APPROVED — 2/2 boards.

### Core proposition
The central order remains legible while meaning, strangeness and devotion accumulate in the margins.

### Distinctive mechanisms
- illuminated-manuscript page architecture;
- marginal illustration as structural behavior;
- decorated initials, seals, chapter marks, dividers and flora/fauna;
- parchment / ink / pigment / gold-leaf materiality;
- contemporary editorial hierarchy inside historical page logic;
- identity capable of working without large hero imagery.

### Hard anti-leak
- no fantasy RPG aesthetic;
- no generic occult symbolism;
- no ornamental overload;
- no blackletter as automatic default;
- no medieval decoration pasted onto unrelated modern grids.

# Global learnings frozen at this checkpoint

1. **Route independence matters.** Mixed-route prompts produced visual contamination and invalid comparisons.
2. **The generator must not solve the full identity in one undifferentiated pass.** Board I and Board II have different jobs.
3. **System evidence is mandatory.** A beautiful hero image is insufficient.
4. **The second board must expand the universe rather than repeat the first hero.**
5. **Oneiric Anatomy currently has the strongest stated pull**, but no route is canonically selected yet.
6. **Safety failures were localized mainly to literal grotesque anatomy**, not to the Brand's surreal / corporeal / dark themes generally.
7. **Liturgical influence works best as structure and behavior**, not as generic moons/stars/occult decoration.
8. **Bestiary imagery is an asset language, not automatically the Brand.**
9. **Darkness must be semantic and selective.** Black alone is not a direction.
10. **Every surviving route must ultimately pass a no-hero / reduced-state identity test.**

# Source-of-truth behavior for the 10 approved boards

The ten approved visual boards are now **positive Phase-5 evidence**.

They may be used to extract principles, baselines and controlled route mutations.

They must **not** be used as permission to:
- blend complete routes without an explicit mutation card;
- copy one board's composition into another route;
- treat generated typography as final font selection;
- treat generated UI/application fragments as final interface design;
- bypass later Signature, Foundations, Composition and Image-Language phases.
