# 2. New explicit preference — SURREALISM

The human owner added an important preference that was underrepresented in earlier documentation: **surrealism is a meaningful attraction and should be explored in combination with the existing DIVINIVID thesis.**

This is not a replacement master tension and does not reopen the frozen plan. It is admitted as a **visual modulation / mechanism family** for Phase 4 and Phase 5.

## Surrealism means, in this program
Prefer:
- dream logic with internal structure;
- symbolic displacement;
- incompatible objects or registers coexisting naturally;
- metaphysical / mental space;
- repetition, doubling and temporal recursion;
- body as sign, not merely specimen;
- quiet uncanny pressure;
- familiar material made impossible without losing compositional discipline.

Avoid:
- random AI weirdness;
- pop-surreal kitsch;
- fantasy RPG logic;
- spectacle for spectacle's sake;
- occult/tarot shorthand;
- gratuitous sexual/body shock;
- incoherent collage.

## Surrealist mechanism references admitted as Phase-4 support
These are mechanism references, not house styles:
- **Odilon Redon** — impossible morphology emerging from atmospheric black;
- **Giorgio de Chirico** — dislocated objects, metaphysical architecture, long shadows and anxiety produced by spatial logic;
- **Maya Deren / `Meshes of the Afternoon`** — dream logic held by elegant structure, repetition, symbolic objects and altered familiar space;
- **The Color of Pomegranates** — ritual symbolic tableau and non-literal object logic, already validated as a high-affinity anchor.

Other surrealist artists/works may be researched during divergence, but they do not become canon by inclusion in a moodboard.
