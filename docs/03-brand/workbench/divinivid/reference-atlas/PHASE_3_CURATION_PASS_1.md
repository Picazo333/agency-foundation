# DIVINIVID — Phase 3 Reference Atlas — Curation Pass 1

## Purpose
This pass converts the broad Phase-2 research universe into a deliberately smaller set of **board survivors**. It does **not** yet promote candidates into the Clean Reference Canon and does **not** close Phase 3.

The governing thesis remains:

> **Contrast with elegance the tension and harmony between the divine and the grotesque, darkness and light, showing how apparent opposites can exist inside the same system.**

## Figma decision for this pass
**DEFER.**

Reason:
- the current task is still conceptual curation, not spatial composition;
- the human board gate has not yet approved survivors;
- premature Figma assembly would spend scarce MCP capacity while also increasing anchoring risk around an arrangement that may still change;
- the approved process requires Figma for final board assembly, not for every intermediate cut.

Figma becomes justified only after human approval of the survivor set or when spatial comparison itself becomes the unresolved question.

## Curation principles
1. Prefer a source with a **unique transferable mechanism** over a famous or visually attractive source.
2. Keep each board between **8–16 references**, but use the lower end when mechanism coverage is already complete.
3. Avoid allowing one source family to dominate multiple boards unless it genuinely carries different mechanisms in each.
4. `INSPIRATION` = concept, atmosphere, narrative or operating principle.
5. `EXECUTION` = page, image, spatial or material behavior that could later inform design construction.
6. `CORE` sources from the Clean Reference Canon remain high-authority, but may still be absent from a specific board if redundant.
7. `PROMOTE CANDIDATE` remains a Phase-3 candidate until the human owner explicitly promotes it.
8. Contemporary copyrighted work remains `REFERENCE_ONLY`; public-domain artwork still requires image-specific rights verification before production reuse.
9. Anti-Canon rules remain hard constraints.

# Board 01 — DIVINE / ILLUMINATION

## Analytical target
Define sacredness without relying on literal religious branding, pseudo-luxury gold, celestial clichés or monochrome solemnity. The board should demonstrate that divine charge can be carried by **light, pigment, scale, material, ritualized composition and revelation**.

## Proposed survivors — 8

### 1. Hours of Catherine of Cleves — ca. 1440
**Role:** `EXECUTION + INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- intense sacred polychromy;
- calm central narrative with inventive margins;
- gold and pigment as meaningful material;
- repeated system with high local variation.

Do not extract:
- ornamental border on every surface;
- blackletter or faux parchment as medieval shorthand.

Primary tensions: `DIVINE↔GROTESQUE`, `ORDER↔ABERRATION`.

Why it survives: strongest source for sacred color + system + margin behavior.

### 2. Isenheim Altarpiece — Grünewald / Haguenau, 1512–1516
**Role:** `INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- sacred luminosity capable of containing difficult flesh;
- radical state change between darkness/suffering and revelation;
- light/color used narratively.

Do not extract:
- wounds or crucifixion imagery as Brand content.

Primary tensions: all five.

Why it survives: most complete historical precedent for the core DIVINIVID thesis.

### 3. The Color of Pomegranates — Sergei Parajanov, 1969
**Role:** `INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- saturated sacred color without loss of gravity;
- frontal tableau;
- material/object as symbolic carrier;
- ritual weight through color blocking.

Do not extract:
- Armenian cultural motifs detached from context.

Primary tensions: `BODY↔TRANSCENDENCE`, `LIGHT↔DARKNESS`.

Why it survives: prevents the false equation `sacred = dark/desaturated`.

### 4. Caravaggio / tenebrism — CORE family
**Role:** `INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- light as semantic revelation;
- body emerging selectively from darkness;
- sacred significance carried by physical flesh and staging.

Do not extract:
- Baroque costume or faux-oil-paint effect.

Primary tensions: `LIGHT↔DARKNESS`, `BODY↔TRANSCENDENCE`.

Why it survives: establishes the rule that illumination must mean something.

### 5. William Blake — Illustrations of the Book of Job, 1826
**Role:** `EXECUTION + INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- divine image, text, border and secondary imagery acting as one semantic page;
- celestial/terrestrial vertical hierarchy;
- narrative architecture contained by disciplined engraving.

Do not extract:
- Blakean angel anatomy as house style.

Primary tensions: `DIVINE↔GROTESQUE`, `ORDER↔ABERRATION`, `KNOWLEDGE↔MYSTERY`.

Why it survives: bridges sacred image to future editorial/system design.

### 6. Bruder Klaus Field Chapel — Peter Zumthor, 2007
**Role:** `INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- darkness as vessel for light;
- rough material ↔ controlled outer form;
- puncture/oculus as revelation.

Do not extract:
- concrete/brutalism as contemporary shorthand.

Primary tensions: `LIGHT↔DARKNESS`, `ORDER↔ABERRATION`.

Why it survives: translates illumination from image into material/space.

### 7. Kwaidan — Masaki Kobayashi, 1965
**Role:** `INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- luminous supernatural atmosphere;
- controlled surrealism;
- designed color worlds that remain unsettling.

Do not extract:
- Japanese folklore motifs out of context.

Primary tensions: `BEAUTY↔UNCANNY`, `ORDER↔ABERRATION`, `LIGHT↔DARKNESS`.

Why it survives: demonstrates that unreality and color can remain disciplined/elegant.

### 8. Gustave Doré — CORE engraving family
**Role:** `INSPIRATION + EXECUTION`

Extract:
- monumental black;
- sublime scale;
- human ↔ vast architecture/nature relationship;
- religious/fantastic gravity without decorative prettiness.

Do not extract:
- make all outputs engraving-heavy.

Primary tensions: `LIGHT↔DARKNESS`, `BODY↔TRANSCENDENCE`.

Why it survives: adds vertical magnitude and the sublime, which the manuscript sources do not provide.

## Board statement
> **The divine in DIVINIVID is not a symbol set. It is a behavior of light, pigment, scale, material and revelation.**

## Explicit cuts from this board
- `Soulages` → stronger and less redundant in Darkness/Revelation and Reconciliation.
- `Macario` → stronger as atmospheric core in Darkness/Revelation.
- `Ronchamp` → useful but redundant with Bruder Klaus for this board.
- `Moreau` → held due decorative/decadent-luxury contamination risk.

# Board 02 — GROTESQUE / CORPOREAL

## Analytical target
Define grotesque as **symbolic anomaly inside a meaningful system**, not gore, fantasy concept art or demon spectacle. The body should remain a site of knowledge, hybridity, pressure, instability or transformation.

## Proposed survivors — 8

### 1. Saint Anthony Tormented by Demons — Martin Schongauer, 1470–74
**Role:** `CORE INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- calm focal body versus chaotic perimeter;
- believable hybrid anatomy;
- grotesque as pressure surrounding order.

Do not extract:
- demon swarm as recurring Brand content.

Primary tensions: `DIVINE↔GROTESQUE`, `ORDER↔ABERRATION`.

Why it survives: cleanest mechanism for `order under grotesque pressure`.

### 2. Aberdeen Bestiary — c. 1200
**Role:** `CORE EXECUTION`

Extract:
- creature as knowledge object;
- illustration + text + moral system;
- precious material paired with taxonomy.

Do not extract:
- animal emblem as automatic logo;
- fantasy/heraldic styling.

Primary tensions: `KNOWLEDGE↔MYSTERY`, `DIVINE↔GROTESQUE`.

Why it survives: strongest source for `classification that remains mythic`.

### 3. Luttrell Psalter — before 1340
**Role:** `EXECUTION`

Extract:
- formal text/sacred hierarchy + grotesque marginal register;
- local irregularity without systemic collapse.

Do not extract:
- medieval comedy as overall tone.

Primary tensions: `ORDER↔ABERRATION`, `DIVINE↔GROTESQUE`.

Why it survives: gives the grotesque a spatial role instead of making it the whole composition.

### 4. Hieronymus Bosch — selective Saint Anthony / grotesque drawings
**Role:** `SUPPORT INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- grotesque micro-events inside larger ordered cosmology;
- hybridization logic;
- anomaly distributed in local zones.

Do not extract:
- `Bosch-like` AI fantasy;
- saturation of every surface with creatures.

Primary tensions: `DIVINE↔GROTESQUE`, `ORDER↔ABERRATION`.

Why it survives: useful only as a controlled reference for distributed anomaly.

### 5. Andreas Vesalius — De humani corporis fabrica, 1543
**Role:** `CORE EXECUTION`

Extract:
- body as structured knowledge;
- figure + label + plate hierarchy;
- cross-hatching and line density as information.

Do not extract:
- gore, medical UI or faux-Latin decoration.

Primary tensions: `BODY↔TRANSCENDENCE`, `KNOWLEDGE↔MYSTERY`.

Why it survives: keeps corporeality anchored to knowledge rather than horror.

### 6. Isenheim Altarpiece — closed / suffering state
**Role:** `INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- difficult flesh remaining inside a sacred system;
- physical disturbance without loss of gravity.

Do not extract:
- wounds as shock device.

Primary tensions: `DIVINE↔GROTESQUE`, `BODY↔TRANSCENDENCE`.

Why it survives: proves corporeal discomfort can remain serious and sacred.

### 7. Odilon Redon — noirs / impossible morphology
**Role:** `INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- ambiguous natural ↔ metaphysical form;
- impossible anatomy emerging from darkness;
- unsettling without explicit gore.

Do not extract:
- floating eye as Brand motif.

Primary tensions: `BODY↔TRANSCENDENCE`, `KNOWLEDGE↔MYSTERY`.

Why it survives: widens grotesque beyond historical monsters and exposed anatomy.

### 8. William Blake — Job / Behemoth & Leviathan register
**Role:** `SUPPORT INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- monster explicitly contained by celestial/page geometry;
- anomaly given a structural location.

Do not extract:
- Biblical creature iconography literally.

Primary tensions: `DIVINE↔GROTESQUE`, `ORDER↔ABERRATION`.

Why it survives: demonstrates containment rather than spectacle.

## Board statement
> **The grotesque in DIVINIVID does not destroy order; it reveals where order stops being sufficient.**

## Explicit cuts from this board
- additional demonology → mechanism saturation and occult/fantasy contamination risk.
- Goya → darkness/void contribution is stronger elsewhere; not needed for corporeal grammar.
- contemporary body-horror → incompatible with the approved `beautiful → strange → unsettling` sequence.

**Role:** `CORE INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- natural darkness and localized illumination;
- metaphysical presence treated matter-of-factly;
- material/rural reality carrying spiritual weight.

Do not extract:
- folklore motifs or vintage-film styling.

Why it survives: strongest existing atmosphere reference for DIVINIVID.

### 2. The Seventh Seal
**Role:** `CORE INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- severe figure/void relationships;
- monumental negative space;
- intellectual sacred/existential symbolism.

Do not extract:
- chess/death iconography.

Why it survives: darkness through scale, landscape and silence.

### 3. The Night of the Hunter — 1955
**Role:** `INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- lyrical image + threat;
- silhouette as psychological/moral structure;
- extreme black/white polarity with emotional meaning.

Do not extract:
- Southern Gothic motifs.

Why it survives: unique fusion of eerie beauty and menace.

### 4. Vampyr — 1932
**Role:** `INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- low-contrast spectral half-light;
- unstable perception;
- supernatural unease through optical behavior.

Do not extract:
- vampire/fog shorthand.

Why it survives: prevents `darkness = black` simplification.

### 5. Caravaggio / tenebrism
**Role:** `CORE EXECUTION PRINCIPLE`

Extract:
- darkness as reserve;
- localized illumination as semantic reveal.

Why it survives: supplies the clearest reveal logic.

### 6. Pierre Soulages — Outrenoir
**Role:** `INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- black as active optical surface;
- relief/texture modulating light;
- material variation inside apparent monochrome.

Do not extract:
- monochrome identity or copied brushwork.

Why it survives: conceptual inversion `black can produce light`.

### 7. Bruder Klaus Field Chapel — 2007
**Role:** `INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- material darkness containing a literal opening to light;
- ritualized vertical orientation.

Why it survives: turns the reveal mechanic into space/process.

### 8. Gustave Doré — CORE engraving family
**Role:** `EXECUTION + INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- monumental black;
- tiny human / vast world scale;
- density versus void.

Why it survives: gives darkness scale rather than merely mood.

### 9. Andrei Rublev — 1966
**Role:** `INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- mud, stone, fire, weather and architecture as lived spiritual matter;
- darkness produced by material/world conditions, not polish.

Do not extract:
- bleakness as prestige coding.

Why it survives: prevents overly graphic or luxurious darkness.

## Board statement

- `SUSPEND`

# Board 04 — ORDER / KNOWLEDGE

## Analytical target
Define the rational skeleton that allows DIVINIVID to contain mystery and anomaly without becoming chaotic. The board must demonstrate that information, taxonomy, annotation, ornament and image can coexist inside a legible system.

## Proposed survivors — 8

### 1. Andreas Vesalius — De humani corporis fabrica, 1543
**Role:** `CORE EXECUTION`

Extract:
- plate hierarchy;
- body + label + folio;
- section/layer as knowledge;
- line density as information.

Why it survives: strongest body-as-knowledge anchor.

### 2. Andreas Cellarius — Harmonia Macrocosmica, 1660/1708
**Role:** `CORE EXECUTION`

Extract:
- diagram as visual event;
- concentric/orbital hierarchy;
- information + labels + frame + historical color.

Do not extract:
- zodiac imagery as occult shorthand.

Why it survives: strongest proof that rigor and polychromy can coexist.

### 3. William Blake — Illustrations of the Book of Job
**Role:** `CORE EXECUTION`

Extract:
- multiple information registers inside one page;
- border/text/image hierarchy;
- celestial/terrestrial structure.

Why it survives: relevant to editorial and later digital information architecture.

### 4. Hypnerotomachia Poliphili — 1499
**Role:** `CORE EXECUTION`

Extract:
- text-image equivalence;
- disciplined page rhythm;
- inscription-derived typography logic;
- calm margin around complex symbolic content.

Do not extract:
- Renaissance antiquarian motifs as decoration.

Why it survives: strongest source for page harmony without card-based modernist defaults.

### 5. Albrecht Dürer — Apocalypse / Four Horsemen, 1498
**Role:** `INSPIRATION + EXECUTION`

Extract:
- directional density;
- hierarchy under collision;
- print-scale monumentality.

Why it survives: teaches controlled maximalism.

### 6. Hours of Catherine of Cleves — ca. 1440
**Role:** `SUPPORT EXECUTION`

Extract:
- repeated architecture with high local variation;
- center ↔ margin role separation.

Why it survives: order need not imply repetition or sterility.

### 7. Aberdeen Bestiary — c. 1200
**Role:** `SUPPORT EXECUTION`

Extract:
- creature + text + taxonomy + moral system;
- knowledge that retains mythic ambiguity.

Why it survives: unique bridge between classification and mystery.

### 8. Robert Fludd — Utriusque Cosmi / microcosm-macrocosm diagrams
**Role:** `SUPPORT INSPIRATION`

Extract:
- body/cosmos correspondence;
- concentric/radial knowledge architecture;
- layered symbolic information.

Do not extract:
- occult/alchemical category cues or sigils.

Why it survives: conceptually valuable bridge for `BODY↔TRANSCENDENCE`, but must remain visually subordinate to Cellarius.

## Board statement
> **The rational system must be rigorous enough to let the irrational appear without making the whole identity chaotic.**

## Explicit cuts from this board
- modern Swiss/grid references → not needed; the canon already rejects grid spectacle and contemporary structure can be solved later at system level.
- Kelmscott Chaucer → powerful but stylistically gravitational and redundant with Hypnerotomachia + manuscript architecture.
- Häxan → classification/grotesque hybrid remains interesting but its sensationalism makes it weaker than Aberdeen + Blake for this board.

# Board 05 — RECONCILIATION

## Analytical target
Study only sources where apparent opposites remain simultaneously valid inside one coherent system. This is the **master board**: it should reveal mechanisms, not merely mood.

## Proposed survivors — 10

### 1. Isenheim Altarpiece
Mechanism: `TRANSFORM + CONTAIN`

Oppositions:
- agony ↔ resurrection;
- difficult flesh ↔ sacred luminosity;
- closed state ↔ open state.

Why it survives: richest total-system precedent.

### 2. Hours of Catherine of Cleves
Mechanism: `CONTAIN`

Oppositions:
- calm sacred center ↔ inventive/strange margin;
- repeated architecture ↔ local variation.

Why it survives: demonstrates coexistence through role separation.

### 3. Saint Anthony Tormented by Demons — Schongauer
Mechanism: `INVADE`

Oppositions:
- serenity ↔ grotesque pressure;
- centered body ↔ chaotic perimeter.

Why it survives: cleanest spatial invasion model.

### 4. The Color of Pomegranates
Mechanism: `RECONCILE`

Oppositions:
- spiritual gravity ↔ saturated color/material excess;
- sacredness ↔ theatrical construction.

Why it survives: proves color intensity can deepen rather than cheapen ritual seriousness.

### 5. Macario
Mechanism: `RECONCILE`

Oppositions:
- everyday material life ↔ metaphysical presence;
- darkness ↔ spiritual clarity.

Why it survives: supernatural and ordinary share one sober world.

### 6. Pierre Soulages — Outrenoir
Mechanism: `REVEAL`

Oppositions:
- black ↔ light;
- monochrome appearance ↔ optical variation.

Why it survives: one material produces its supposed opposite.

### 7. Bruder Klaus Field Chapel
Mechanism: `CONTAIN + REVEAL`

Oppositions:
- rough darkness ↔ precise luminous opening;
- massive enclosure ↔ upward release.

Why it survives: physical/spatial reconciliation.

### 8. Andreas Cellarius — Harmonia Macrocosmica
Mechanism: `MIRROR`

Oppositions:
- science/diagram ↔ ornament/polychromy;
- rigor ↔ visual splendor.

Why it survives: rejects the false split between information and beauty.

### 9. William Blake — Book of Job
Mechanism: `CONTAIN + RECONCILE`

Oppositions:
- divine order ↔ Satan/monster/suffering;
- image ↔ text;
- celestial ↔ terrestrial.

Why it survives: multiple conceptual registers held by one page architecture.

### 10. Bill Viola — The Crossing, 1996
Mechanism: `TRANSFORM`

Oppositions:
- body ↔ opposing forces;
- stable anchor ↔ transformation;
- presence ↔ disappearance/aftermath.

Why it survives: makes duality temporal rather than merely compositional.

## Board statement
> **DIVINIVID does not juxtapose opposites. It designs the mechanism that allows both to be true at once.**

## Six reconciliation operations extracted
1. `CONTAIN` — one register can hold an opposing register without losing hierarchy.
2. `INVADE` — anomaly enters an ordered field while the field remains legible.
3. `REVEAL` — one pole makes the other visible through contrast/material behavior.
4. `MIRROR` — apparently different domains share an underlying structure.
5. `TRANSFORM` — the relationship is understood through state change over time.
6. `RECONCILE` — the composition demonstrates that the opposition was never absolute.

These operations are **Phase-3 synthesis candidates**, not yet route grammar canon.

# Anti-Atlas — candidate structure

## Purpose
Make the main failure families concrete without treating adjacent styles as inherently bad. Each category must identify the shortcut that conflicts with DIVINIVID.

## Ten anti-directions
1. `PSEUDO_LUXURY` — black + gold + Didone/tracking used to simulate sacred/premium value.
2. `AI_GOTHIC` — generalized gothic darkness, random cathedral/angel/demon imagery and synthetic detail without system.
3. `OCCULT_SHORTCUT` — tarot/sigils/moons/eyes/zodiac used without source-specific meaning.
4. `STATUE_SERIF_BLACK` — classical bust + serif + black field as generic seriousness.
5. `TRENDY_BRUTALIST_AGENCY` — huge type, Swiss/grid spectacle, fashionable concrete imagery.
6. `DARK_ACADEMIA` — library/candle/book/coffee/sepia used as instant intellectual atmosphere.
7. `FANTASY_MEDIEVAL` — game-concept demons, armor, cathedral CGI, boss aesthetics.
8. `CYBER_DARK` — cyan/neon/glow/particles/AI-future gradients.
9. `COLORLESS_PSEUDO_MEDIEVAL` — beige parchment + blackletter + distressed texture that erases historical polychromy.
10. `MEDICAL_FUTURISM` — glossy 3D anatomy, holograms/HUDs/X-ray-cyan visual language.

## Anti-Atlas image rule
Do not spend Figma/image-generation effort creating synthetic examples during this curation pass. If the human approves the ten categories, final Atlas assembly may use:
- carefully sourced real-world examples where rights/source documentation is practical; or
- minimal schematic demonstrations created directly in Figma when a real external brand example would create unnecessary rights/attribution noise.

The anti-image must illustrate the **failure mechanism**, not imitate or ridicule a specific competitor.

# Figma trigger criteria

Figma is required when **one or more** of the following becomes true:
1. the human approves/mutates the survivor lists and asks to see spatial relationships;
2. unresolved quality depends on image scale, adjacency, rhythm, density or negative space rather than source selection;
3. Visual Asset Curation needs to judge the set as a visual whole;
4. final board assembly and board-to-board differentiation need evaluation;
5. an Anti-Atlas failure mechanism is easier to communicate with a schematic than prose.

Figma is **not** required for:
- another source-name cut;
- rights/provenance bookkeeping;
- `EXTRACT / DO NOT EXTRACT` refinement;
- board-role clarification;
- textual quality gate review.

Given the current Starter/View MCP constraints, this trigger policy is both quality-preserving and resource-conscious.
