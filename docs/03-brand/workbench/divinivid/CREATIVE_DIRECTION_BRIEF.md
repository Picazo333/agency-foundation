# DIVINIVID — Creative Direction Contract

> **Phase:** 1 / incomplete until the human creative owner closes the Phase 1 gate.
>
> This document starts from already approved intent. It is not permission to generate a new identity board before the remaining operational definitions are completed.

## 1. Emotional target — approved center
DIVINIVID should feel intellectually serious, visually arresting and emotionally resonant without performing darkness for its own sake. Encountering it should create an initial sense of beauty/order, expose an anomaly or unsettling truth, and then reveal that both belong to the same coherent world.

The intended experience is **elegant tension**, not compromise. The divine must not sanitize the grotesque; the grotesque must not trivialize the divine.

## 2. Direction in one line
> **Sacred order capable of containing bodily, symbolic and metaphysical anomaly without losing grace.**

## 3. Master tensions
- Divine ↔ Grotesque
- Light ↔ Darkness
- Order ↔ Aberration
- Knowledge ↔ Mystery
- Body ↔ Transcendence

## 4. Working definitions to formalize during Phase 1

### Divine
**Working meaning:** revelation, transcendence, sacred proportion, luminous significance, ritual seriousness, iconographic charge.

**Not:** church-brand cliché, generic halo, luxury gold, celestial particles, tarot shorthand.

### Grotesque
**Working meaning:** meaningful deformation, hybridity, marginalia, bodily strangeness, symbolic anomaly, historical monster/bestiary intelligence.

**Not:** gore as spectacle, shock for shock's sake, videogame demon design, gross-out imagery.

### Elegance
**Working meaning:** deliberate hierarchy, restraint where restraint increases force, precise relationships, strong negative space when needed, disciplined material/color use, no visual element without a role.

**Not:** minimalism by default, luxury-brand tropes, sterilization, fashionable emptiness.

### Darkness
**Working meaning:** atmosphere, concealment, scale, silence and selective revelation.

**Not:** making every surface black, generic dark mode, edge-lord styling.

### Illumination / light
**Working meaning:** semantic revelation; light or pigment appears because something deserves to be seen.

**Not:** glow effects, neon, generic gradients.

### Historical
**Working meaning:** source intelligence, authentic material/visual mechanisms and culturally situated references.

**Not:** cosplay, distressed filters, blackletter-by-default, Pinterest medievalism.

### Contemporary
**Working meaning:** current composition discipline, digital behavior, information architecture, production precision, accessibility and responsive behavior.

**Not:** trendy brutalism, SaaS cards, cyber-tech, Swiss-grid spectacle or whatever is fashionable this quarter.

## 5. Directional axes — to close in Phase 1

### Tone Register
Working center: **measured / precise with controlled provocation**.

Decision still required: define when provocation is allowed and when restraint dominates.

### Aesthetic Philosophy
Working center: **controlled maximalism inside disciplined hierarchy**.

Decision still required: define the boundary between rich information/materiality and over-ornamentation.

### Audience Relationship
Working center: **peer + authority** — sophisticated enough to reward close attention, clear enough not to become self-indulgent.

Decision still required: specify how this changes across brand, proposal, research and product surfaces.

### Sensory Ambition
Working center: **resonant**, not merely considered. The work should be felt, but function cannot be sacrificed.

Decision still required: define the maximum intensity appropriate to commercial/B2B contexts.

## 6. Budgets / limits — Phase 1 deliverable
These are not arbitrary percentages. They must become observable rejection rules.

### Ornament budget
To define: where ornament may accumulate; minimum quiet zones; when marginalia becomes noise.

### Pigment budget
To define: how ultramar/carmine/gold and secondary historical pigments interrupt or inhabit ink/ivory structures; when color becomes decorative.

### Darkness budget
To define: which contexts may be dominantly dark, which require luminous/light states, and how the Brand avoids becoming synonymous with black.

### Grotesque budget
To define: acceptable intensity by context — internal art/campaign, public brand page, proposal, product/UI, favicon/micro-mark.

### Historical visibility budget
To define: when provenance should be visibly historical versus abstracted into contemporary grammar.

## 7. Narrative operation
Working sequence:

`REVEAL → DISTURB → RECONCILE`

Alternative temporal formulations may be tested later, but they must preserve the thesis rather than become decorative animation.

## 8. Non-negotiable rejections
- cyan/neon/cyberpunk/gaming aesthetics;
- AI-purple / generic futurist gradients;
- glassmorphism;
- dark-luxury black/gold shorthand;
- occult-shop / tarot-brand shortcuts;
- fantasy RPG / demon concept art;
- generic dark academia;
- stock “premium agency” aesthetic;
- trend brutalism as identity;
- anatomy as gore or decoration;
- generic serif + statue + black + star;
- modernist/grid aesthetic dominating the visual personality;
- colorless pseudo-medievalism that erases illuminated-manuscript polychromy.
