# DIVINIVID — Creative Direction Contract

> **Phase:** 1 / CLOSED 2026-09-16
>
> **Human instruction:** the creative owner explicitly instructed the Phase 1 execution on 2026-09-16. This contract closes the phase after the written gate tests below pass. It does not approve any logo, typeface, palette value, generated image, route artifact or Brand Direction V0.

---

# 1. Project read

## 1.1 Brand
`DIVINIVID` is the Brand under identity-system exploration. `KIROGRAF` is Route 01 internal metadata and `Sacred Anatomy` is its internal descriptor.

## 1.2 Creative thesis
> **DIVINIVID contrasts with elegance the tension and harmony between the divine and the grotesque, darkness and light, demonstrating how apparent opposites can coexist inside one system.**

The Brand is not a compromise between poles. The desired result is a legible, deliberate coexistence in which each pole increases the force of the other.

## 1.3 Master tensions
1. `DIVINE ↔ GROTESQUE`
2. `LIGHT ↔ DARKNESS`
3. `ORDER ↔ ABERRATION`
4. `KNOWLEDGE ↔ MYSTERY`
5. `BODY ↔ TRANSCENDENCE`

## 1.4 Design read
> **Reading DIVINIVID as a contemporary Brand system for serious creative/commercial use, with a measured and intellectually authoritative tone, controlled-maximalist visual language, peer-to-authority audience relationship, and resonant sensory ambition — built from historical source intelligence without historical cosplay.**

## 1.5 Intended encounter
The preferred perceptual sequence is:

`BEAUTY / ORDER → ANOMALY / DISTURBANCE → RECONCILIATION`

The temporal shorthand remains:

`REVEAL → DISTURB → RECONCILE`

A successful DIVINIVID artifact should usually have an identifiable first reading, a second layer that complicates it, and a final structure that makes the tension feel intentional rather than accidental.

# 2. Operational meanings

These definitions are rejection criteria, not mood words.

## 2.1 Divine
**Means:** significance beyond utility; revelation; transcendence; sacred proportion; ritual seriousness; luminous or iconographic charge; an object, body, text or space treated as worthy of sustained attention.

**Observable signals:** deliberate hierarchy; semantic illumination; ceremonial spacing or sequence; monumental scale relationships; iconographic concentration; material care.

**Does not mean:** generic halos; celestial particles; church-brand clichés; automatic crosses; gold-as-sacred shorthand; tarot/occult marks; vague spirituality.

**Reject when:** sacredness can be removed without changing the artifact's meaning because it exists only as decoration.

## 2.2 Grotesque
**Means:** meaningful anomaly inside a coherent order — deformation, hybridity, marginalia, ambiguous anatomy, impossible proportion, strange historical imagination, symbolic disturbance.

**Observable signals:** an ordered system containing something that violates expectation while remaining conceptually related to that system.

**Does not mean:** gore, disgust, shock bait, videogame demons, horror-poster effects, photorealistic AI monsters.

**Reject when:** the anomaly exists only to look dark, edgy or shocking.

## 2.3 Elegance
**Means:** exact relationships, decisive hierarchy, disciplined contrast, material economy, intentional negative space, and the removal of anything that cannot justify its role.

Elegance is the **control system** that allows density, strangeness and historical richness without collapse.

**Does not mean:** minimalism by default; luxury-brand emptiness; thin serif + gold; visual sterilization; polite blandness.

**Reject when:** richness has no hierarchy, or restraint has removed the tension that makes DIVINIVID distinct.

## 2.4 Beauty
**Means:** formal coherence, craft, proportion, material pleasure, color/light intelligence and a perceptible order that invites attention before the work complicates that attention.

Beauty is not required to be comforting. In DIVINIVID it often provides the doorway through which strangeness becomes tolerable enough to inspect.

**Does not mean:** prettiness, decorative nostalgia, luxury polish, symmetry for its own sake.

**Reject when:** beauty suppresses anomaly or becomes a generic premium finish.

## 2.5 Horror / uncanny
**Means:** a controlled recognition that something familiar is wrong, impossible, doubled, displaced, revealed or too close to the body; tension that produces attention rather than spectacle.

**Preferred intensity:** psychological, symbolic, bodily or spatial unease before explicit horror.

**Does not mean:** blood, mutilation, jump-scare logic, horror franchise aesthetics, gore typography.

**Reject when:** the unsettling quality becomes the primary entertainment value.

## 2.6 Darkness
**Means:** concealment, silence, depth, scale, uncertainty and the condition that makes selective revelation meaningful.

Darkness may be literal value, negative space, withheld information, slow disclosure or a materially heavy field.

**Does not mean:** every surface is black; generic dark mode; black-to-purple gradients; edge-lord styling.

**Reject when:** the Brand cannot remain recognizable on a predominantly light/ivory surface.

## 2.7 Illumination / light
**Means:** semantic revelation. Light, white space, pigment or contrast appears because something deserves to be seen or understood.

**Observable signals:** localized light; selective saturated pigment; focal contrast; revealed body/diagram/text; controlled emergence from darkness.

**Does not mean:** neon glow; bloom; lens flare; generic gradient; random bright accents.

**Reject when:** light/pigment has no relationship to hierarchy or meaning.

## 2.8 Restraint
**Means:** deliberately withholding visual intensity so the next concentrated event has force. Restraint is pacing, not absence.

**Observable signals:** calm fields; unornamented reading zones; limited simultaneous focal events; pauses between dense regions; typography allowed to function plainly when required.

**Does not mean:** converting the identity into austere minimalism.

**Reject when:** either every area is activated or every area is neutralized.

## 2.9 Controlled maximalism
**Means:** high local richness governed by a clear hierarchy and surrounded by enough quiet structure to remain legible. Density is concentrated, not uniformly sprayed across the surface.

A controlled-maximalist composition normally contains:
1. one dominant reading/focal event;
2. one subordinate system of detail or annotation;
3. at least one intentionally quiet field or reading zone.

**Does not mean:** fill every margin; stack ornamental systems; make every module unique; decorate every card.

**Reject when:** removing one decorative layer improves clarity without damaging meaning, or when more than one region competes for primary attention without a deliberate narrative reason.

## 2.10 Historical
**Means:** using culturally situated source mechanisms, material logic, composition, pigmentation, annotation, anatomy, image-making and ritual structure with traceable provenance.

Historical presence can be explicit or abstracted, but the source mechanism must be explainable.

**Does not mean:** costume; distressed filters; blackletter; parchment on everything; generic Gothic; beige medievalism; artist imitation.

**Reject when:** a historical look is present but no source mechanism or provenance can be named.

## 2.11 Contemporary
**Means:** current production precision, responsive behavior, accessibility, information architecture, scalable system logic, digital motion, device awareness and the ability to operate in present-day commercial contexts.

Contemporary is a **behavioral and systems property**, not a trend style.

**Does not mean:** brutalist template, Swiss-grid spectacle, SaaS bento, glassmorphism, cyber-tech, current fashion mimicry.

**Reject when:** contemporaneity is being communicated mainly through a fashionable visual trope rather than through how the system behaves.

# 3. Directional axes — resolved

## 3.1 Tone Register
**Center:** `PROFESSIONAL / MEASURED` with a bounded `PROVOCATIVE` edge.

The Brand speaks with precision first. Provocation is allowed when it exposes a contradiction, names an uncomfortable truth, juxtaposes incompatible-seeming material, or creates productive intellectual friction.

### Provocation is allowed when
- it clarifies a master tension;
- the audience can understand what is being challenged;
- the statement/image remains conceptually precise;
- the provocation earns a second reading rather than merely producing shock.

### Restraint dominates when
- presenting evidence, pricing, scope, contractual information or operational instructions;
- a user is performing a task;
- ambiguity would reduce trust;
- the visual environment is already intense.

### Hard fail
Provocation that is decorative, antagonistic without purpose, adolescent, pseudo-occult or dependent on shock language.

---

## 3.2 Aesthetic Philosophy
**Center:** `CONTROLLED MAXIMALIST`.

DIVINIVID may be rich in material, image, annotation and historical information, but must never become uniformly dense. The identity works through **contrast between concentrated richness and disciplined quiet**.

### Boundary rule
A composition may be dense only if all three are true:
1. a primary hierarchy remains obvious at first reading;
2. secondary detail rewards closer inspection rather than competing with the primary reading;
3. a calm zone exists that allows the eye, text or task to recover.

### Hard fail
If density is evenly distributed, if every module receives ornament, or if the visual must be explained before hierarchy becomes visible, it has crossed into uncontrolled maximalism.

---

## 3.3 Audience Relationship
**Center:** `PEER ↔ AUTHORITY`.

DIVINIVID does not patronize the audience and does not perform mystery to hide weak reasoning. Authority comes from specificity, source intelligence, craft and evidence; peer relationship comes from respecting the audience's intelligence.

### Surface behavior
| Surface | Relationship center | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Brand / manifesto / editorial | Peer with selective authority | Invite interpretation, but make the thesis legible. Mystery may remain after meaning is established. |
| Proposal / sales / procurement | Authority through clarity and specificity | Reduce visual mystery; state scope, evidence, tradeoffs and obligations directly. |
| Research / insight / case study | Authority + transparent reasoning | Annotation and evidence are visible; separate observation from interpretation. |
| Product / tool / operational UI | Companion-functional with quiet authority | The task is protagonist. Brand tension appears through system details, not by obstructing use. |
| Campaign / cultural artifact | Peer-provocateur | Highest permission for ambiguity, confrontation and layered symbolism, while preserving the Brand thesis. |

### Hard fail
Mystique used as a substitute for explanation; self-important authority; interface copy that behaves like a manifesto when the user needs an answer.

---

## 3.4 Sensory Ambition
**Center:** `RESONANT`, with intensity reduced according to task criticality.

The Brand should create a specific emotional charge, not merely look considered. However, sensory ambition is subordinate to comprehension and task success.

### Ceiling by surface
- **Campaign / cultural / hero moments:** full resonant ambition is permitted.
- **Brand/editorial:** resonant, but narrative pacing must preserve readability.
- **Proposal / sales / research:** considered-to-resonant; evidence and legibility win conflicts.
- **Product / operational UI:** functional-to-considered; resonance appears in transitions, type hierarchy, material accents, illustration moments and macro-composition, never in core task friction.

### Hard fail
Animation, darkness, ornament, texture, distortion or historical styling that slows a critical action, obscures information, weakens accessibility or makes the surface feel like a portfolio piece instead of a usable object.

# 4. Visual intensity budgets

Budgets are qualitative operating constraints, not arbitrary percentages.

## 4.1 Ornament budget

### O0 — none
Pure functional layer: forms, dense tables, legal text, utility controls, tiny marks.

### O1 — trace
One restrained structural device: rule, folio, frame fragment, marginal marker, inscription cue, small emblematic interruption.

### O2 — composed
A primary ornamental system plus subordinate detail. Suitable for brand/editorial and selected proposal moments.

### O3 — ceremonial
Rich border/marginalia/illumination or multi-layer material construction. Reserved for hero, campaign, cultural and deliberately art-directed moments.

### Rules
- Ornament attaches to meaning: hierarchy, boundary, narrative, source logic or semantic emphasis.
- Body text, data, forms and primary CTAs require an O0/O1 reading zone even inside an O2/O3 artifact.
- Do not apply ornamental frames to every card/module.
- Only one primary ornamental grammar may dominate a composition. Secondary ornament must be visibly subordinate.
- O3 cannot be the default state of the Brand system.

**Reject if:** ornament survives only as decoration, destroys the calm field, or makes every region equally precious.

---

## 4.2 Pigment budget

### P0 — structural neutral
Ink/charcoal/ivory/white only, used to prove the system does not depend on color.

### P1 — semantic accent
One high-signal pigment family enters for meaning or hierarchy.

### P2 — paired historical tension
Two pigment families may coexist when they establish a deliberate contrast or source-derived relationship.

### P3 — polychrome illumination
Multiple historically plausible pigment relationships may coexist in a source-led illuminated/campaign moment, provided hierarchy remains legible.

### Rules
- Ultramarine, carmine/vermilion and aged gold are source territories, not mandatory simultaneous accents.
- Gold behaves as a material/ritual accent, not an automatic luxury color.
- Pigment should reveal, classify, interrupt or transform. If removing the color does not change hierarchy or meaning, it is probably decorative.
- Functional semantic colors required by UI may exist outside the expressive pigment system, but must be harmonized and accessible.
- No generic multicolor gradients; no cyan/neon route.
- Every core identity proof must include a P0 monochrome test.

**Reject if:** the Brand reduces to black/ivory/burgundy pseudo-luxury, or if saturated pigment appears everywhere with no semantic hierarchy.

---

## 4.3 Darkness budget

### D0 — luminous
Predominantly ivory/light field. Darkness is local structure or text.

### D1 — balanced
Light and dark fields coexist; neither is the sole identity condition.

### D2 — dominant darkness
Dark field with localized semantic illumination. Suitable for cinematic/hero/editorial/campaign moments.

### D3 — near-void
Large darkness/negative space with very limited revelation. Reserved for rare, high-intent narrative moments.

### Rules
- Every core identity proof must demonstrate at least one D0/D1 application and one D2 application.
- Proposal, research and operational interfaces default to D0/D1 unless a concrete reason justifies D2.
- D3 cannot carry dense information or critical actions.
- Darkness may never be the only mechanism producing seriousness.

**Reject if:** the Brand fails on ivory/light surfaces, every mockup defaults to black, or darkness substitutes for composition.

---

## 4.4 Grotesque-intensity budget

### G0 — absent
No grotesque content; structure alone carries the Brand.

### G1 — anomaly
Subtle deformation, asymmetry, marginal creature, ambiguous crop, unexpected bodily/diagrammatic relation.

### G2 — legible grotesque
Hybridity, impossible anatomy or clearly strange symbolic/body content is visible but not dominant.

### G3 — confrontational grotesque
The anomaly becomes a principal narrative event. Permitted only in art-directed campaign/editorial/cultural moments.

### G4 — spectacle / gore
Blood, mutilation, shock-first body horror, videogame demon spectacle. **Prohibited globally.**

### Surface ceilings
| Surface | Ceiling |
|---|---|
| Favicon / micro-mark | G0–G1 |
| Product / operational UI | G0–G1 |
| Proposal / procurement / evidence page | G0–G1 |
| Public Brand / editorial | G1–G2, with rare isolated G3 narrative moments |
| Campaign / cultural artifact / internal art study | G2–G3 |

**Reject if:** grotesque intensity exceeds the surface ceiling, becomes gore, or the Brand requires monsters to remain recognizable.

---

## 4.5 Historical-reference visibility budget

### H0 — abstracted rule
The historical source is not visually obvious; only its mechanism survives: hierarchy, light logic, annotation, material behavior, rhythm or composition.

### H1 — trace
A knowledgeable viewer may sense manuscript/engraving/plate/ritual influence, but the artifact reads as contemporary first.

### H2 — legible historical register
Historical material or syntax is visible and intentional, with traceable source logic.

### H3 — explicit source encounter
A named/reproduced historical object, plate, manuscript, engraving or archival artifact is visibly present with provenance/rights context.

### Surface ceilings
| Surface | Normal range |
|---|---|
| Signature / micro-mark | H0–H1 |
| Product / operational UI | H0–H1 |
| Proposal / research | H0–H1; H2 only when source/evidence is relevant |
| Brand / editorial | H1–H2 |
| Campaign / cultural / research feature | H2–H3 |

### Rules
- Whole-style imitation is prohibited at every level.
- H3 requires explicit source/provenance handling.
- Historical visibility must decrease when it competes with comprehension or makes the system read as costume.

**Reject if:** the artifact can be summarized as “make it look medieval/Gothic/like [artist]” rather than as a DIVINIVID system using transferred mechanisms.

# 5. Cross-budget interaction rules

Budgets must not all peak simultaneously by default.

1. **If Ornament = O3, at least one of Grotesque or Historical Visibility should normally remain below its maximum.** Richness needs hierarchy.
2. **If Grotesque = G3, typography and interaction should simplify.** The anomaly is the event.
3. **If Darkness = D3, pigment becomes revelation rather than decoration.** Limit simultaneous competing accents.
4. **If Historical Visibility = H3, contemporary system behavior must be especially clear.** Provenance and present-day hierarchy prevent pastiche.
5. **If Pigment = P3, darkness need not also be maximal.** Medieval polychromy is a positive source, not something to bury in black.
6. **Core identity assets must survive O0/P0/G0/H0 tests where applicable.** The Brand cannot depend entirely on decoration, color, monsters or historical imagery.

# 6. Rejection matrix

An artifact must `MUTATE` or `KILL` if any of the following is true:

- `DIVINIVID` is replaced publicly by `KIROGRAF`;
- it could plausibly belong to a generic “AI creative agency” with only the name changed;
- it uses `serif + statue + black + star` as the primary identity mechanism;
- black/gold performs seriousness or sacredness without deeper logic;
- medieval means beige parchment / blackletter / distressed edges;
- grotesque means gore, photoreal AI monsters or fantasy-game demons;
- anatomy is decorative rather than structural, epistemic, bodily or revelatory;
- historical style is imitated without source-specific mechanism/provenance;
- contemporary is represented mainly by brutalist, Swiss-grid, bento or glass trends;
- ornament has no semantic role;
- every region is equally dense;
- color has no hierarchy or semantic function;
- the work only succeeds on a dark background;
- the work only succeeds with a hero image;
- a critical action or evidence surface sacrifices usability for atmosphere;
- an unapproved generated artifact is used as a positive source;
- the composition cannot explain which master tension it is expressing;
- a designer cannot name what should be removed when intensity exceeds the budget.

# 8. Hypothetical artifact gate tests

No visual was generated for these tests. They test whether the written contract can decide plausible future cases.

## Test A — “premium sacred agency” hero
**Hypothesis:** black full-bleed hero, elegant high-contrast serif, classical marble statue, gold geometric star, faint grain, centered `DIVINIVID`.

**Verdict:** `KILL`.

**Written reasons:** Anti-Canon `serif + statue + black + star`; pseudo-luxury black/gold; darkness performing depth; no master-tension mechanism; historical object as decoration; generic premium-agency substitutability.

## Test B — proposal cover / commercial document
**Hypothesis:** ivory field, precise contemporary typographic hierarchy, one small carmine folio/section marker, subtle anatomical section line used to organize the proposal structure, no hero imagery, no grotesque figure.

**Verdict:** `PASS` provisionally.

**Written reasons:** surface stays inside O0–O1 / P0–P1 / D0–D1 / G0–G1 / H0–H1; anatomy has structural/information role; restraint preserves authority; Brand remains identifiable without darkness or historical spectacle.

**Condition:** the anatomical line must materially organize information rather than become decorative flourish.

## Test C — case-study / research feature
**Hypothesis:** predominantly ivory reading surface with a dark interstitial chapter, ultramarine used only to reveal a key evidence layer, engraved anatomical crop with captions/provenance, small marginal hybrid creature appearing only at a narrative transition.

**Verdict:** `PASS` provisionally.

**Written reasons:** D0/D2 contrast makes illumination semantic; P1 establishes hierarchy; H2 is justified by evidential source use; G1 anomaly is subordinate; contemporary reading structure remains primary.

## Test D — public brand manifesto with photoreal AI demon
**Hypothesis:** cinematic black background, hyperreal horned demon emerging from smoke, red glow, Latin inscription, animated particles.

**Verdict:** `KILL`.

**Written reasons:** fantasy/game demon; photoreal AI horror; glow/particle defaults; grotesque exceeds conceptual requirement; sacred/historical language used as shorthand; no knowledge/body/order mechanism.

## Test E — polychrome illuminated campaign poster
**Hypothesis:** bright ivory ground, saturated ultramarine and carmine with aged-gold material logic, a disciplined central inscription, marginal hybrid anatomy and cosmological geometry, no faux parchment distress, clear contemporary type hierarchy.

**Verdict:** `MUTATE → potential PASS`.

**Written reasons:** P3/O3/H2/G2 are allowed in campaign work, but multiple high budgets are simultaneously active. The composition must reduce one competing layer, preserve a calm field, and prove one dominant focal event before passing.

# 9. Two-method gate review

The Phase 1 gate requires written criteria strong enough that two reviewers can reject off-brief work without relying on preference. This review uses two separate approved methodology lenses. They are independent **review methods**, not two independent human people; final human authority remains with the creative owner.

## Reviewer A — Creative Direction method
**Question:** do the four axis decisions and explicit budgets make downstream choices materially different?

- Test A: `KILL`
- Test B: `PASS`
- Test C: `PASS`
- Test D: `KILL`
- Test E: `MUTATE`

**Result:** PASS. The contract distinguishes surface relationships and intensity ceilings rather than describing one undifferentiated “vibe.”

## Reviewer B — Taste anti-default / canon-drift method

### DESIGN_READ
Serious contemporary Brand identity with historical source intelligence, controlled maximalism and deliberate sacred/grotesque tension; not generic dark-luxury, occult, AI-tech or templated editorial.

### STRONGEST_SIGNAL
The strongest differentiator is not any single motif. It is the **systemic coexistence of order and anomaly**, reinforced by semantic illumination, source-grounded materiality and the ability to move between light and dark states.

### GENERIC_OR_AI_DEFAULTS — traps now explicitly blocked
- centered dark hero as default;
- AI purple/cyan/glow;
- serif = premium;
- statue = culture;
- gold = sacred;
- three-card/bento template identity;
- uniform dark mode;
- photoreal AI monsters;
- historical texture without source logic;
- maximalism as indiscriminate density.

### CANON_DRIFT
No unresolved drift detected in the contract. The remaining open work belongs downstream: Phase 2 reference research, exact imagery, typeface choice, palette values, signature design and route selection.

### CORRECTIONS APPLIED IN THIS CONTRACT
- “controlled maximalism” converted from adjective into a three-part hierarchy rule;
- “provocative” bounded by surface and purpose;
- “peer + authority” split by surface;
- darkness forced to prove light-state viability;
- grotesque given explicit intensity levels and ceilings;
- historical presence given visibility levels and provenance rules;
- pigment separated from generic accent-color decoration;
- multi-budget peak interaction rules added to prevent ceremonial overload.

### KILL_OR_KEEP
`KEEP` the central thesis, five tensions, semantic-light logic, historical polychromy, anatomy-as-knowledge, controlled grotesque and contemporary behavioral layer.

`KILL` any future direction whose distinctiveness collapses to dark luxury, Gothic/occult shorthand, generic AI agency design, historical cosplay or shock imagery.

**Result:** PASS.
