## Central creative thesis
> **Contrast with elegance the tension and harmony between the divine and the grotesque, darkness and light, showing how apparent opposites can coexist inside one system.**

## Master tensions
- Divine ↔ Grotesque
- Light ↔ Darkness
- Order ↔ Aberration
- Knowledge ↔ Mystery
- Body ↔ Transcendence

## Current visual objective
Develop DIVINIVID as a contemporary identity that can combine surrealism, sacred/liturgical structure, corporeal or organic matter, editorial knowledge systems and semantic darkness without collapsing into gore, occult branding, generic Gothic, dark luxury, fantasy concept art, dark-tech, Swiss-grid spectacle or historical pastiche.

# Phase 5 — Current status

**ACTIVE — FIVE-ROUTE VISUAL EXPLORATION CHECKPOINT PASSED.**

The previous drift-reconciliation work successfully led to a new controlled visual exploration. Five independent routes were generated one at a time, with route isolation enforced after mixed prompts caused contamination.

## Frozen two-board rule
Every visual route requires two approved complementary boards before it may enter convergence:

1. **Moodboard I — Identity / Atmosphere**
2. **Moodboard II — System / Surface**

Moodboard II must expand the route into materials, typography in use, web/interface fragments, composition grammar, graphic devices, image treatment, light/dark behavior, motion, reduced state and anti-patterns. It must not merely repeat Moodboard I's hero.

## Approved route set — 10 boards total

### R1 — Dark Revelation
**APPROVED 2/2.**
Semantic darkness, veil, threshold, apparition, negative space and selective revelation.

### R2 — Bestiary of Systems
**APPROVED 2/2.**
Impossible non-human organic systems, classification, annotation, archive and editorial containment.

### R3 — Sacred Corporeal
**APPROVED 2/2.**
Intact human body as sacred threshold, axial presence, halo/veil/materiality and non-traumatic corporeality.

Safety clarification: literal dissection / exposed tissue / graphic anatomy is excluded from this route because it is unnecessary to the thesis and caused avoidable generation failures.

### R4 — Oneiric Anatomy
**APPROVED 2/2.**
Body as dream-logical landscape of meaning: surreal overlap between human form, architecture, archive, symbol, flora and impossible spatial continuity.

**Human signal:** strongest explicit current inclination. This is not yet the final Brand Direction selection.

### R5 — Illuminated Marginalia
**APPROVED 2/2.**
Illuminated-manuscript page logic, marginal illustration, decorated initials, seals, flora/fauna, pigment and contemporary editorial structure.

## Key learning from the five-route run
- route isolation is mandatory;
- full-route mixing before convergence creates aesthetic soup;
- hero imagery is insufficient without system evidence;
- the second moodboard must demonstrate design behavior, not merely aesthetic repetition;
- Oneiric Anatomy currently has the strongest stated pull;
- literal grotesque anatomy was the recurring safety bottleneck, not surrealism, darkness, sacred imagery or organic matter generally;
- liturgical behavior succeeds when structural rather than ornamental;
- Bestiary/specimen language is an asset/system mechanism, not automatically the Brand;
- generated typography and web fragments remain provisional evidence, not final Brand assets.

The ten approved boards are now positive Phase-5 evidence and may be used to extract route DNA and controlled mutations.
