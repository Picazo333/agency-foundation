# DIVINIVID — Working Identity Universe Checkpoint

## Checkpoint declaration

A materially stronger convergence signal emerged from the controlled mutation round:

`R5-D1 — Illuminated Marginalia × Oneiric Displacement`

The human owner identified R5-D1 as the strongest generation produced to date and explicitly promoted it to the **working visual anchor for subsequent exploration**.

This does **not** yet make R5-D1 the final Brand Direction. It makes it the currently preferred lineage from which the visual universe is being expanded and stress-tested.

## Working identity thesis

The current working identity combines:

- contemporary illuminated-manuscript / codex structure;
- editorial-liturgical page logic;
- marked oneiric / surreal displacement;
- narrative marginalia and peripheral meaning;
- ivory / black / crimson / aged-gold material language;
- central order invaded by controlled strangeness;
- symbolic, historical and organic references translated into a contemporary identity;
- mystery with editorial discipline rather than occult branding or fantasy spectacle.

### Short formula

`CONTEMPORARY MANUSCRIPT + LITURGICAL EDITORIALITY + ONEIRIC DISPLACEMENT + NARRATIVE MARGINS + DISCIPLINED SURREALISM`

## What is frozen from R5-D1

- page / codex architecture;
- active margins;
- center ↔ periphery tension;
- surrealism as structured dream logic rather than random collage;
- liturgical influence as a structural mechanism;
- marfil / negro / carmesí / oro envejecido as the current material family;
- narrative fragments, small scenes and symbols that expand the world;
- editorial seriousness;
- richness without fantasy-RPG or occult-brand collapse;
- ability to function without one mandatory hero specimen.

## What remains open

- darkness budget;
- ornament budget;
- typographic final families;
- degree of human/corporeal presence;
- digital density;
- motion behavior;
- final wordmark/signature;
- exact palette/tokens;
- degree of historical visibility;
- system simplification at operational scales.

# New frozen process rule

A promising direction may not enter mutation as a single hero image alone.

Once a generation is promoted to **working identity anchor**, it should first be expanded into a bounded identity-universe proof sufficient to understand:
- type;
- color/material;
- image;
- composition;
- digital/motion;
- reduced state;
- applications/stress behavior.

For this lineage, the required proof is now complete: **7/7 approved boards**.

## Parent hierarchy from now on

Future mutations should treat the **system extracted from R5-D1 + the seven approved Identity Universe boards** as the parent.

The single R5-D1 image remains the origin artifact, but future generations must not copy its composition literally or reduce the Brand back to one codex spread.

---

# Current anti-drift rules

1. Do not reopen free route discovery.
2. Do not silently merge complete prior routes.
3. Every mutation changes declared dimensions only.
4. Do not let generated type become final font selection.
5. Do not let a generated web mockup become final UI.
6. Do not let medieval visibility become historical costume.
7. Liturgical structure must remain functional, not pasted ornament.
8. Surrealism must remain meaningful and controlled.
9. No cyan / neon / dark-tech.
10. No fantasy RPG / occult branding / stock-surreal.
11. No graphic anatomical gore.
12. The Brand must remain recognizable at reduced, light, monochrome and no-hero states.
