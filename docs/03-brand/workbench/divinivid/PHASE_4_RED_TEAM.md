# DIVINIVID — Phase 4 Tension Grammar Red Team

## Purpose
Stress-test `DIVINIVID_VISUAL_GRAMMAR_V0.md` before any original route generation begins.

The grammar passes only if it can generate a distinct, usable identity system without depending on named-source imitation, dark styling, historical imagery, or one hero composition.

This is a failure-seeking document. A visually attractive result does not override a structural failure.

---

# 1. Red-team principles

1. Attack the grammar, not the taste of a single artifact.
2. Prefer tests that expose hidden dependency on source imagery.
3. Treat genericity as a system failure even when execution quality is high.
4. Separate `creative tension` from `visual noise`.
5. Separate `surreal mechanism` from `random weirdness`.
6. Separate `anatomical order` from `anatomy wallpaper`.
7. Require both expressive and functional survivability.
8. A route that only works as a large-format hero is not yet a Brand system.

---

# 2. Core attacks

## RT01 — Source removal
Remove every historical/cinematic reference image.

**Pass if:** hierarchy, operation logic, annotation behavior, tension and material language still feel specific.

**Fail if:** the work becomes a generic serif-on-black agency system.

## RT02 — Monochrome
Force P0 monochrome.

**Pass if:** structure, contrast, bodily logic and composition still carry identity.

**Fail if:** pigment is doing all the conceptual work.

## RT03 — Light-surface inversion
Move the composition from darkness-dominant to ivory/light-dominant.

**Pass if:** DIVINIVID remains recognizable through line, hierarchy, body, framing, annotation and tension.

**Fail if:** identity requires black background to exist.

## RT04 — Small-format compression
Reduce to mobile card / slide thumbnail / social tile scale.

**Pass if:** one primary event and hierarchy survive.

**Fail if:** meaning depends on tiny engraving detail or excessive marginalia.

## RT05 — No-body test
Remove figurative anatomy/body imagery.

**Pass if:** operation logic still works through geometry, framing, inscription, material, aperture, annotation or motion.

**Fail if:** anatomy is a crutch rather than a grammar.

## RT06 — Anatomy substitution
Replace Vesalian imagery with a neutral contemporary scientific diagram.

**Pass if:** the artifact retains `ANATOMICAL ORDER` behavior through structure rather than stylistic quotation.

**Fail if:** the route only works when it visually resembles Vesalius.

## RT07 — Surreal subtraction
Remove explicit surreal imagery but retain relational dislocation.

**Pass if:** oneiric tension remains through scale, repetition, context, spatial contradiction or sequence.

**Fail if:** surrealism is only a moodboard look.

## RT08 — Surreal escalation
Increase dream logic one step beyond the proposed axis profile.

**Pass if:** hierarchy and solemnity survive.

**Fail if:** output becomes whimsical, random, kitsch, meme-like, fantasy, or AI-weird.

## RT09 — Grotesque escalation
Increase G intensity by one step.

**Pass if:** anomaly remains interpretable and subordinate to the system.

**Fail if:** grotesque becomes spectacle, gore, monster art, or shock-first content.

## RT10 — Ornament deprivation
Remove borders, decorative frames, textures and historical ornament.

**Pass if:** the route still reads through operations and hierarchy.

**Fail if:** ornament is carrying identity by itself.

# 3. Genericity attacks

## RT11 — Luxury substitution
Replace body/diagram content with a fashion object while keeping typography/background.

**Fail if:** the result still feels equally plausible. That means the system is pseudo-luxury rather than DIVINIVID.

## RT12 — Gothic substitution
Replace references with generic cathedral/angel imagery.

**Fail if:** the route accepts the substitution without losing specificity.

## RT13 — Agency-template substitution
Place the same type hierarchy into a trendy design-studio template.

**Fail if:** nothing essential breaks.

## RT14 — Cyber substitution
Apply cyan glow, scanlines or HUD marks.

**Expected:** immediate visible incompatibility. If the route absorbs them naturally, its grammar is under-specified.

## RT15 — Occult substitution
Replace source-derived symbols with eye/sigil/zodiac/tarot shorthand.

**Expected:** rejection. If meaning remains unchanged, symbolism is too generic.

---

# 4. System-fertility attacks

## RT16 — Three-surface transfer
Apply one route to:
1. expressive hero;
2. editorial/research page;
3. low-imagery operational/business surface.

**Pass if:** one grammar produces all three without costume changes.

## RT17 — Repetition fatigue
Generate three artifacts from one route without reusing the same hero motif.

**Pass if:** identity survives through rules rather than repeated imagery.

## RT18 — Information stress
Add a real title, subtitle, body copy, caption, metadata, CTA and navigation state.

**Pass if:** mystery does not destroy information architecture.

## RT19 — Accessibility pressure
Require readable contrast and reduced-motion behavior.

**Pass if:** visual identity does not depend on illegible low contrast, tiny labels or motion-only revelation.

## RT20 — Brand-removal recognition
Hide the word `DIVINIVID` and route name.

**Pass if:** a reviewer can still describe a coherent visual logic close to the Brand thesis.

# 5. Operation-specific attacks

## CONTAIN
Failure signature: frame exists but anomaly is arbitrary decoration.

## INVADE
Failure signature: global grunge/texture with no source, direction or target.

## REVEAL
Failure signature: cinematic lighting with no interpretive change.

## MIRROR
Failure signature: symmetry is formal but conceptually empty.

## TRANSFORM
Failure signature: morphing for spectacle; no invariant survives.

## RECONCILE
Failure signature: tension is neutralized into generic harmony.

## DISLOCATE
Failure signature: unrelated dream objects accumulate without a legible expectation being violated.

# 6. Human-affinity checks

The owner does not need to justify taste academically. The following signals are valid data:

- `PULL` — wants to keep looking / feels personally resonant;
- `CURIOUS` — concept is fertile but not yet emotionally convincing;
- `CRAFT ONLY` — execution is good but does not feel like the Brand;
- `REPEL` — wrong direction despite technical quality;
- `OVERDONE` — too dramatic, grotesque, surreal, ornate or historical;
- `UNDERDONE` — too clean, generic, timid or corporate.

These signals do not replace structural evaluation; they complement it.

---

# 7. Gate matrix

Phase 4 can PASS when:

- all seven operations have explicit failure signatures;
- anatomy survives the substitution test;
- surrealism survives both subtraction and escalation tests;
- dark and light surfaces are both viable;
- at least one route can work with no source image;
- the grammar can transfer across hero/editorial/operational surfaces;
- generic luxury/Gothic/occult/cyber substitutions are visibly incompatible;
- the owner approves the grammar as the basis for iterative visual discovery.

## Decision semantics
- `PASS` — proceed to Phase 5.
- `MUTATE` — revise only failed grammar rules, axes or route profiles.
- `KILL` — remove an operation or route hypothesis that cannot survive the relevant attacks.
