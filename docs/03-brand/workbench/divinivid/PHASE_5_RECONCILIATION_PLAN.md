# DIVINIVID — Phase 5 Reconciliation Plan

## Why this document exists
Phase 5 produced useful human-affinity evidence, but later rounds drifted from the frozen method. The problem was not simply aesthetic quality: generated artifacts began to recycle morphology, change multiple variables at once, mix route exploration with downstream identity applications, and let full-board generations behave as if they were design systems.

This document reconciles the work without discarding the valid learning.

The immediate rule is:

> **No further image generation until the surviving positive evidence, negative evidence, capability routing and success criteria below are accepted as the active Phase-5 contract.**

---

# 1. Positive evidence retained

## P1 — Oneiric Anatomy / OA-01
Human signal: strong original pull.

Preserve as evidence, not as mandatory form:
- solemn oneiric charge;
- body / interior / threshold relation;
- crimson as semantic event rather than decorative accent;
- ivory / darkness tension;
- monumental but controlled pictorial presence.

Do not preserve automatically:
- human figure as required subject;
- cathedral-like internal architecture;
- exact composition;
- any generated morphology.

## P2 — Bestiary of Systems / BS-01
Human signal: strong original pull.

Preserve:
- impossible organism treated seriously;
- archive / classification / evidence logic;
- refined editorial containment;
- specimen as a carrier of meaning rather than fantasy character;
- system potential beyond one hero image.

Do not preserve automatically:
- bilateral silhouette;
- exact dossier structure;
- exact creature anatomy.

## P3 — BS-04 Surreal Dislocation
Human signal: strongest later mutation signal.

Preserve:
- clearly marked surrealism rather than timid surreal accents;
- organic impossible matter;
- creature / organism logic that feels dream-born rather than designed as fantasy concept art;
- credibility of an observing / classifying system despite impossible behavior.

This is currently the strongest mechanism-level signal from Round B.

## P4 — Dark Revelation affinity
Human signal: desired elegance and atmosphere.

Preserve as an atmospheric mechanism:
- semantic darkness;
- controlled revelation;
- silence / restraint;
- apparition rather than spectacle;
- significant negative space.

Do not treat this as permission to converge routes before system proof.

## P5 — Liturgical illustration affinity
Human signal: explicit request to restore what later rounds lost.

Preserve as a visual-language lane:
- manuscript / marginalia / devotional illustration logic;
- ceremonial framing;
- sacred-image seriousness;
- illuminated / inscribed detail used structurally, not as decorative Gothic wallpaper.

# 2. Negative evidence and failure modes

The following are not source material for future generation. They are retained only to prevent repetition.

## N1 — Sequential visual contamination
Symptom: separate iterations became visibly too similar, especially when one successful composition implicitly anchored the next.

Failure:
- mutation labels differed;
- structural output did not differ enough;
- generated artifact became an undeclared reference.

Correction:
- generated artifacts cannot seed later visual generation unless the human owner explicitly names the transferable principle;
- prompts must resolve from grammar + approved source principles, not from prior generated pixels.

## N2 — Morphology lock / repeated dragon-wyvern solution
Symptom: multiple identity boards repeated essentially the same reptilian / winged morphology with superficial changes.

Failure:
- the generator solved “oneiric beast” once and reused the solution;
- board diversity became layout diversity, not concept diversity.

Correction:
- subject morphology becomes a controlled variable;
- if a divergence round tests multiple hypotheses, silhouette/family/posture cannot repeat unless repetition itself is the declared experiment;
- outputs failing morphology independence are `KILL` regardless of craft quality.

## N3 — Full-board generation masquerading as identity design
Symptom: the generator simultaneously invented creature, typography, layout, liturgical ornament and web/application fragments.

Failure:
- too many variables changed at once;
- generated typography looked plausible but was not a validated type system;
- applications appeared before foundations were frozen;
- attractive board craft obscured weak identity logic.

Correction:
- image generation supplies isolated visual ingredients and hypothesis evidence;
- editable assembly / comparison becomes the design task;
- typography shown during Phase 5 is role/behavior evidence only, never a font selection;
- application mockups are reserved for system proof and later phases.

## N4 — Inconsistent comparison contract
Symptom: some iterations showed brand applications, some only creatures, some dense dossiers, some pure hero art.

Failure: human review compared unlike evidence.

Correction: every comparison set must share a declared evidence contract before generation.

## N5 — Liturgical layer decay
Symptom: as bestiary/surrealism increased, the earlier manuscript / sacred-illustration signal weakened or disappeared.

Correction: liturgical illustration becomes an explicit tracked lane rather than a vague “ritual” score.

## N6 — Safety-driven wording distorted art direction
Symptom: prompts around corporeality risked graphical-body interpretations and forced repeated reformulations.

Learning:
- the desired effect does not require injury;
- use non-traumatic anatomical, skeletal, tendinous, membranous, botanical, radiographic, atlas and impossible-morphology language;
- never let safety reformulation silently change the conceptual question being tested.

Use after every generation batch to judge the set together, produce final-pick and cut rationales, and reject outliers that break the visual system.

### Brand Identity — authority: identity-system methodology
Use to ensure later proof covers signature, color, typography, imagery and system behavior rather than treating imagery as the identity itself.

When quota/access is available, comparison boards and approved visual ingredients must be assembled there. The image generator is not the source of truth.

# 4. Revised execution model

## Stage R0 — Reconcile and freeze evidence
Complete this document, Negative Evidence Ledger, control-state update and PR review.

Exit condition:
- positive evidence is named;
- invalid generations are quarantined;
- current survivor lineage is explicit;
- next experiment has a single declared question.

## Stage R1 — Independent visual-ingredient probes
Do not generate full identity boards.

Generate isolated families that answer one question each, for example:
- organism morphology;
- oneiric physical behavior;
- dark-revelation lighting;
- liturgical illustration / marginalia treatment;
- scientific / editorial annotation language.

Each probe is judged as a set using Visual Asset Curation.

Hard rule: no probe may invent typography, website layout and organism simultaneously.

## Stage R2 — Survivor assembly
Assemble approved ingredients into a controlled comparison surface.

Required components:
- one primary image language sample;
- one secondary illustration / liturgical sample;
- provisional typographic-role strip using neutral/placeholder families if necessary;
- composition skeleton;
- explicit annotation/information behavior.

The assembly must be editable; generated pixels do not determine the layout.

## Stage R3 — Phase-5 system proof
Resume the existing Iteration Contract Round C with up to two surviving lineages.

For each lineage, prove exactly three comparable surfaces:
1. Expressive Hero;
2. Editorial / Research Surface;
3. Reduced / Operational Surface.

Typography at this stage proves hierarchy and register only. Final font choice remains Phase 8.

## Stage R4 — Convergence gate
Only after R3 may two lineages be combined.

A convergence proposal must name:
- mechanism inherited from lineage A;
- mechanism inherited from lineage B;
- rejected traits from both;
- why the combined grammar survives all three surfaces better.

# 6. Success criteria for the next visual round

A new generation batch is valid only when all pre-generation conditions pass.

## Pre-generation gate
- one question only;
- one primary variable only;
- explicit constants;
- explicit morphology constraints if a creature is involved;
- source principles named from canon / grammar;
- no prior generated image used as visual seed unless explicitly approved;
- output type fixed across the comparison set;
- no premature final typography or application design.

## Batch success criteria
A batch passes only if:
1. **Independence:** variants are structurally distinguishable at thumbnail size.
2. **Question fidelity:** differences answer the declared variable, not unrelated changes.
3. **Human affinity:** at least one result produces real pull, not merely craft admiration.
4. **Anti-generic:** no fantasy-concept-art, AI Gothic, pseudo-luxury, dark-tech or occult-default collapse.
5. **Organic specificity:** “oneiric beast” cannot default to dragon/wyvern morphology.
6. **System potential:** the visual language can plausibly yield more than one hero picture.
7. **Liturgical compatibility:** when tested, sacred/manuscript logic integrates structurally rather than as ornament pasted around the organism.
8. **Dark-revelation compatibility:** darkness reveals meaning instead of simply making the image blacker.
9. **Safety stability:** non-traumatic wording preserves the intended aesthetic question.
10. **Traceability:** every accepted signal is recorded before another generation begins.

Any set with cosmetic variation but repeated underlying form fails even if visually polished.

Do not begin with four identity boards.

Begin with a morphology-independent **Oneiric Organic Organism Probe** for the primary lineage.

Question:
> What families of impossible organic form preserve the pull of BS-04 without collapsing into dragon/wyvern/fantasy anatomy?

Constants:
- solemn;
- elegant;
- organic;
- clearly oneiric;
- non-traumatic;
- ivory / black / crimson-compatible;
- no sci-fi gloss;
- no cliche occultism;
- no typography, layout or website mockup inside the generated image.

The probe must create meaningfully different morphological families. Only after one or more families receive human `PULL` do we separately test Dark Revelation and Liturgical Illustration against those approved mechanisms.

This isolates the exact bottleneck that caused the failed rounds.
