## Working aesthetic definition

### Short formula

`LIVING DARKNESS + LIVING CRIMSON MATTER + CONTEMPORARY CODEX + SURGICAL EDITORIAL DISCIPLINE + CONTROLLED ONEIRISM`

### Design thesis

DIVINIVID should feel like an **atlas of impossible phenomena**: visually deep, organic, dream-logical and ritual, but observed and presented with the precision of scientific plates, museum conservation records or surgical documentation.

The identity's tension is not chaos versus order as separate modes. It is:

> **extraordinary matter, presented with extraordinary discipline.**

## Why WB2 was promoted

The owner preferred WB2 because it achieved a stronger balance than both the preceding crimson-heavy mutation and the later explicit MUT-C board.

Key strengths:

1. **Darkness behaves as material**, not simply background.
2. **Crimson behaves as living substance**, but is not over-literal or blood-like.
3. **Editorial order is severe and clean**, creating useful contrast with the surreal content.
4. **Ornament is reduced**, so the strange matter carries more meaning.
5. **Scientific/surgical observation appears as a presentation framework**, not as a competing aesthetic.
6. **Surrealism remains present but controlled.**
7. **The board suggests transformable states** such as `condense / veil / emerge / disperse`, creating future motion and interaction opportunities.
8. The system can plausibly support product/app presentation, research surfaces, brand storytelling and derived entity systems.

## OPEN / later systems

The following ideas are approved as **derived-system opportunities**, but should not be allowed to redefine the core aesthetic before WB2 is stable:

### Motion motif
`blood-cell-like microform → condensation → crimson disc → red moon`

Use as poetic transformation, not literal hematology.

### Bestiary Entity System
A catalog/family of brand entities or mascots:
- strongly medieval illustration language;
- each entity treated as part of a coherent catalog;
- not the master Brand aesthetic;
- potentially used for narrative, sections, features, campaigns or system states.

### Scientific / Surgical Display Framework
Use scientific-plate discipline to present:
- product/app features;
- systems;
- research;
- case studies;
- components;
- workflows;
- states and transformations.

The creative imagery stays rich; the **presentation layer stays precise**.

### Living Darkness
Darkness may:
- absorb;
- conceal;
- invade;
- contain;
- reveal;
- behave as a field or substance.

### Living Crimson
Crimson may:
- emerge;
- pulse;
- condense;
- disperse;
- transform;
- connect microscopic and cosmic scales.

## Anti-drift

Do not:
- revert to literal erythrocyte wallpaper;
- make blood/gore the visual concept;
- replace disciplined editorial structure with collage saturation;
- let scientific formatting become sterile biotech;
- turn medieval entities into fantasy-RPG mascots;
- make ornament compete with the living matter;
- overuse celestial symbols as generic occult shorthand;
- revert to cyan/neon/dark-tech;
- flatten darkness into ordinary black backgrounds.
