# DIVINIVID — Surface Lock V1

## Purpose
Prevent technical board topics from changing the approved dark execution surface.

The A16-A20 drift incident proved that statements such as "Living Darkness is the default" were too interpretive for generation. This file converts the final approved surface into explicit execution constraints.

## 1. Default canvas dominance

Unless an exact contract explicitly requires a light/print study:
- near-absolute black / Living Darkness should occupy roughly **75-90% of the total canvas**;
- ivory/light fields should normally remain **bounded inserts, labels, specimen windows, captions or controlled counterforms**;
- a board must not become ivory-dominant merely because its subject is accessibility, governance, factory readiness, typography, diagrams or documentation.

### Fail condition
If the first-glance gestalt reads as "ivory editorial atlas with dark accents" rather than "dark DIVINIVID field with controlled ivory counterforms", reject before human review.

## 2. A/B/H surface behavior

### A — Dramatic / Revelatory
- dark field remains dominant;
- stronger imagery and crimson events;
- larger transformations;
- ivory remains local, not the canvas default.

### B — Abyssal / Subtle
- black dominance increases further;
- imagery becomes sparse and low-amplitude;
- gold and crimson are restrained;
- never convert B into cream/ivory luxury minimalism.

### H — Hybrid
- default remains dark;
- local light surfaces may appear only where information or specimen logic benefits;
- context changes amplitude, not the underlying field.

## 3. Ivory rules

Ivory may be:
- typography;
- annotation;
- specimen field;
- contained editorial insert;
- light-reveal state;
- controlled print simulation when the contract explicitly requires it.

Ivory may not:
- become the automatic board background;
- wash out the dark universe;
- serve as shorthand for "accessible", "technical", "scientific" or "premium";
- exceed the dark field simply because a board contains matrices, diagrams or documentation.

## 4. Human/anatomical figure rules

Do not use a human face, bust, anatomical cutaway or monumental figure as the default hero.

Allowed only when:
- the exact contract requires entity/anatomy/human-scale evidence; and
- the figure performs a semantic role such as transformation, embodiment, threshold, scale or analogy.

Reject if it functions only as:
- solemnity;
- sacredness;
- sophistication;
- "premium editorial";
- a visual placeholder.

## 5. Architecture/celestial rules

Architecture and celestial forms are conditional assets, not default mood.

They are allowed only when:
- required by the exact contract; or
- they demonstrate threshold, scale, transformation, environment, impossible geometry or celestial/bestiary logic.

Reject:
- decorative arches;
- cathedral mood filler;
- decorative moon/eclipse;
- ritual geometry that carries no system meaning.

## 6. Gold and crimson surface constraints

### Living Crimson
Use as active matter/event/rupture/transformation. It should feel embedded in the system.

### Aged gold
Use as instrument, measurement, hierarchy or controlled liturgical order.

Reject:
- gold as a broad luxury wash;
- black + gold as the identity by itself;
- crimson as a decorative burgundy accent.

## 7. Pre-display surface QA

Before a board can be shown:
1. thumbnail test: does the board read dark at first glance?
2. ivory takeover test: did a technical topic turn the surface light?
3. statue/human-shortcut test;
4. architecture/celestial-shortcut test;
5. semantic-color test;
6. execution-anchor similarity test: does tonal gravity remain compatible with A11-A15 without copying their content?

Possible outcomes:
- `SURFACE_PASS`
- `SURFACE_MUTATE`
- `SURFACE_REJECT`

No board failing this surface gate reaches the human as a valid candidate.
