## What these anchors lock

Preserve across future boards unless the exact contract explicitly requires a justified exception:
- near-absolute Living Darkness as the dominant field;
- deep black continuity across the canvas;
- ivory as contained counterform, type, labels, specimen fields or bounded inserts rather than default background;
- Living Crimson embedded as matter/event/rupture, not decorative burgundy;
- aged gold restricted to micro-instrumentation, hierarchy and ritual/measurement accents;
- visual depth produced by matter, void, scale and selective illumination;
- dark editorial composition with surgical information order;
- dreamlike imagery contained inside a disciplined system;
- asymmetry and negative space without drifting into generic luxury minimalism;
- no automatic sacred-premium human/statue hero.

## What these anchors do NOT authorize
A recurring motif in an approved execution anchor is not automatically a universal ingredient.

Do not copy:
- a specific portal just because A12 contains one;
- a reliquary just because A13 contains one;
- a celestial body just because A15 contains one.

The exact contract decides content. The anchors decide **surface treatment, tonal gravity, composition discipline and rendering character**.

## Generation reference rule

For each board:
1. load 2-3 execution-style anchors from this set;
2. load only the principle/semantic Canon anchors required by the exact contract;
3. never use quarantined A16-A20 or off-plan web/mobile generations as references.

If the generation system supports explicit image references, pass only approved execution-style anchors + exact contract-specific Canon anchors. Do not rely on recent conversation imagery.

## Critical rule
**Latest approved execution style outranks older positive references for surface treatment. STYLE-S01-S05 are not program-contract IDs and must never advance the 100-board cursor.**

Older Canon anchors retain semantic authority, but they may not reintroduce an obsolete or exploratory surface treatment into the current execution phase.
