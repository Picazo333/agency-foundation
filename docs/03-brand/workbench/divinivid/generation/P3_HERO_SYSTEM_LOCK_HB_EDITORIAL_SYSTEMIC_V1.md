- pure atmospheric revelation; or
- conventional commercial landing-page behavior.

- matter remains primary and ambiguous;
- information hierarchy is visible;
- composition feels editorial and architectural;
- typography retains weight;
- commercial meaning can be layered in without flattening the universe.
