# DIVINIVID Image / Illustration Language V1

## 0. Purpose

Turn the already-approved visual matter into controlled, reproducible families.

This is a classification and production contract.
It does not create a new aesthetic route or authorize volume generation.

Future asset production should no longer need to rediscover what counts as native DIVINIVID matter.

## 1. Global image law

A native DIVINIVID image should normally satisfy at least three of these conditions:
- supports more than one credible scale/read;
- behaves as evidence, threshold, transformation or active void;
- can coexist with editorial hierarchy;
- contains a material logic rather than a decorative texture;
- preserves ambiguity without becoming random AI-weirdness;
- remains compatible with Living Darkness / ivory / Living Crimson / aged-gold / residual ultramar semantics;
- can be cropped or sequenced without losing all identity.

Image language is not defined by anatomy alone.

## 2. Global treatment overlays

These are cross-family treatments, not independent image families.

### Printed Evidence
From B1:
- layered registration;
- plate/impression logic;
- erosion/transfer;
- archival trace;
- tactile substrate;
- controlled incompleteness.

Use as evidence of process/material.
Do not simulate random vintage distress globally.

### Negative Shape / Invisible Body
From B1:
- figure revealed through absence;
- active negative space;
- presence inferred from missing matter;
- material defines void; void defines material.

Use as composition/reveal logic.
Do not convert every asset into a silhouette trick.

### Macro / Micro Ambiguity
A crop may plausibly read as:
- body and landscape;
- tissue and celestial structure;
- geology and marrow;
- membrane and architecture.

Literal identity should often remain delayed.

## 3. Asset provenance / rights state

Family grammar does not itself grant usage rights.

Every production asset must carry one of:
- `GENERATED_ORIGINAL`;
- `LICENSED`;
- `PUBLIC_DOMAIN_VERIFIED`;
- `OWNED_CAPTURE`;
- `REFERENCE_ONLY_DO_NOT_PUBLISH`.

Unknown rights state = not production-ready.

Source/reference imagery may teach a mechanism without becoming a publishable asset.

Generated assets begin as `EVIDENCE_PENDING` until curated and approved.

## 4. Family contracts

---

## 4.1 POROUS MARROW / BONE-LIKE

### USE WHEN
- the concept involves interiority, structure, fragility, threshold or living evidence;
- a surface needs tactile biological/mineral ambiguity;
- a close crop can operate across body/geology scales.

### DO NOT USE WHEN
- it would read as literal medical bone photography;
- a clean functional surface does not need visual matter;
- the concept relies on gore, injury or shock.

### CROP
Prefer:
- macro sections;
- edge-to-void transitions;
- partial structures;
- ambiguous internal cavities.

Avoid:
- textbook anatomical framing;
- full identifiable skeletal parts as automatic motif.

### LIGHT
Directional or localized reveal.
Deep cavities may remain unresolved.

### COLOR
Ivory/bone and darkness dominate.
Crimson only as semantic rupture/interiority.
Gold rarely, as evidentiary/instrument relation rather than material coating.

### DETAIL
Medium-to-high locally; never uniformly sharp across the full frame.

### MATERIAL
Porous, mineral, calcified, sedimentary, fibrous.

### TREATMENT
Printed evidence and controlled erosion are compatible.

### GENERATION / SOURCE REQUIREMENTS
Prompts/briefs must request ambiguous organic/mineral structure, not “gothic bone art”.
Reject literal skeleton shorthand unless explicitly required.

### RIGHTS STATE
Per-asset tag required.

### FAILURE MODE
Medical stock, gore, skull/gothic shorthand, beige vintage anatomy collage.

---

## 4.2 GEOLOGICAL STRATA / SEDIMENT

### USE WHEN
- expressing accumulated knowledge, time, layered systems, pressure or evidence;
- a composition needs lateral or sectional rhythm.

### DO NOT USE WHEN
- it becomes generic marble/stone luxury texture;
- geology has no conceptual relationship to the content.

### CROP
Favor sectional cuts, faults, discontinuities, interfaces and scale ambiguity.

### LIGHT
Raking or localized light that reveals layer structure.

### COLOR
Darkness/ivory base.
Crimson may behave as fault/pressure/event.
Residual ultramar is permitted only as rare depth trace.

### DETAIL
Layer-specific; some bands can dissolve into void.

### MATERIAL
Sediment, strata, fault, accretion, mineral trace.

### TREATMENT
Registration offsets may emphasize distinct layers if they remain controlled.

### GENERATION / SOURCE REQUIREMENTS
Avoid stock “rock texture”.
Require a readable layer mechanism and ambiguous scale.

### RIGHTS STATE
Per-asset tag required.

### FAILURE MODE
Decorative stone slab, corporate marble, fantasy landscape.

---

## 4.3 CELESTIAL MASS

### USE WHEN
- macro/micro duality is central;
- the image needs scale uncertainty, transcendence or remote material presence;
- ceremonial or contemplative modes require a singular mass.

### DO NOT USE WHEN
- it turns into generic space art, nebula wallpaper or sci-fi UI;
- blue/cyan atmospheric color dominates.

### CROP
Partial orb/mass, horizonless close crop, off-frame scale, body-like surface.

### LIGHT
Localized revelation from darkness; avoid uniform glow.

### COLOR
Living Darkness dominant.
Ivory or bone highlight.
Ultramar residual only.
Crimson only if it changes interpretation as matter/event.

### DETAIL
Large quiet fields with localized dense surface evidence.

### MATERIAL
Planetary / tissue / mineral ambiguity, not literal astronomy illustration.

### TREATMENT
Negative revelation strongly compatible.

### GENERATION / SOURCE REQUIREMENTS
Prompt for material ambiguity, not stars/nebula spectacle.

### RIGHTS STATE
Per-asset tag required.

### FAILURE MODE
NASA-poster imitation, cyan sci-fi, fantasy planet concept art.

---

## 4.4 MEMBRANE / VOID

### USE WHEN
- absence, threshold, concealment or permeability is the conceptual mechanism;
- active negative space must carry identity;
- motion will reveal through withdrawal rather than insertion.

### DO NOT USE WHEN
- a membrane becomes a generic liquid/blob effect;
- void is merely black background.

### CROP
Edges, apertures, cavities, interrupted boundaries, partial enclosures.

### LIGHT
Light should reveal the boundary or what the absence implies.

### COLOR
Darkness and ivory/bone.
Crimson may mark rupture/transition.
Avoid luminous neon edge treatments.

### DETAIL
High near thresholds; low in the void.

### MATERIAL
Skin-like, mineral membrane, paper/plate edge, cavity, negative imprint.

### TREATMENT
Negative Shape is the primary compatible overlay.

### GENERATION / SOURCE REQUIREMENTS
Require structure and threshold; prohibit liquid morphing aesthetics.

### RIGHTS STATE
Per-asset tag required.

### FAILURE MODE
Blob art, smoke wallpaper, generic abstract black texture.

---

## 4.5 TECTONIC / VASCULAR CRIMSON

### USE WHEN
- crimson has a semantic role: rupture, flow, transformation, pressure, activation;
- a system needs a clear event within otherwise restrained matter.

### DO NOT USE WHEN
- crimson is being added only to make the Brand recognizable;
- the entire composition becomes red atmosphere;
- blood/gore is the first read.

### CROP
Networks, faults, channels, directional invasions, localized transitions.

### LIGHT
Crimson can carry the focal contrast but must still coexist with readable hierarchy.

### COLOR
Use locked crimson roles.
Dark-surface signal and material values must obey R2.
Do not invent additional reds casually.

### DETAIL
Medium; structure must be legible enough to imply direction/source.

### MATERIAL
Vascular / tectonic / fissure / pigment invasion ambiguity.

### TREATMENT
INVADE, Registration and Transform are compatible.

### GENERATION / SOURCE REQUIREMENTS
Brief must name what the crimson event means.

### RIGHTS STATE
Per-asset tag required.

### FAILURE MODE
Blood effect, horror poster, red-black gamer aesthetic, brand accent pasted everywhere.

---

## 4.6 FIBROUS / BRANCHING MATTER

### USE WHEN
- interconnection, growth, propagation or delicate structure is conceptually relevant;
- the surface needs a lighter counterpoint to heavy masses.

### DO NOT USE WHEN
- it resembles generic neural-network AI art;
- it becomes botanical decoration;
- it reads as electricity/lightning.

### CROP
Partial networks, edge entries, layered depth; avoid centered symmetrical “tree” forms by default.

### LIGHT
Directional and selective.
Fine branches need sufficient contrast without neon glow.

### COLOR
Ivory/darkness preferred.
Crimson can mark one active branch system.
Ultramar remains rare.

### DETAIL
High locally, with surrounding quiet.

### MATERIAL
Fiber, vessel, root-like, fracture, filament—without locking into one literal category.

### TREATMENT
Printed transfer and controlled registration compatible.

### GENERATION / SOURCE REQUIREMENTS
Avoid “neural network”, “synapse”, “cyber organic” prompt shortcuts.

### RIGHTS STATE
Per-asset tag required.

### FAILURE MODE
AI-network cliché, botanical vintage, lightning VFX.

---

## 4.7 TOPOGRAPHIC / COASTAL AMBIGUITY

### USE WHEN
- the system needs map/body/material ambiguity;
- sectional or evidence-led compositions need a scalable surface;
- Editorial/Systemic or Anatomical/Topographic mode is active.

### DO NOT USE WHEN
- it becomes literal GIS decoration;
- fake contour labels/coordinates are added;
- the reading is only “map”.

### CROP
Coast-like edges, nested contours, large-to-small transitions, incomplete boundaries.

### LIGHT
Primarily contrast through material/void rather than cinematic illumination.

### COLOR
Darkness / ivory / bone.
Crimson as event or boundary only.
Gold instrumentation only when a real data/measurement relationship exists.

### DETAIL
Multi-scale; macro contour plus micro evidence.

### MATERIAL
Map / tissue / coastline / growth / erosion ambiguity.

### TREATMENT
Printed evidence and Negative Shape strongly compatible.

### GENERATION / SOURCE REQUIREMENTS
No invented cartographic metadata.

### RIGHTS STATE
Per-asset tag required.

### FAILURE MODE
Fake map interface, arbitrary coordinates, decorative contour wallpaper.

---

## 4.8 SCULPTURAL / FIGURE PRESENCE

### USE WHEN
- human/figure presence materially advances the narrative;
- body-as-symbol or threshold is required;
- a source/production brief can avoid classical-statue shorthand.

### DO NOT USE WHEN
- the figure is merely a prestige prop;
- the composition would work identically with any marble bust;
- it introduces eroticization, gore or fantasy character design;
- it drifts toward dark-luxury editorial.

### CROP
Prefer partial body, silhouette/absence, sectional presence, non-heroic scale ambiguity.

### LIGHT
Tenebrist/localized revelation with semantic purpose.

### COLOR
Usually restrained.
Crimson should mark event/transformation rather than costume/decoration.

### DETAIL
Enough for recognition; preserve controlled ambiguity in setting/material relation.

### MATERIAL
Living / relic / specimen / architectural relation.

### TREATMENT
Negative Shape, Reveal and Dislocate may be used if hierarchy remains rigorous.

### GENERATION / SOURCE REQUIREMENTS
A figure brief must state its narrative role.
“Classical statue in black space” is rejected by default.

### RIGHTS STATE
Portrait/model/source releases as applicable; otherwise per-asset tag required.

### FAILURE MODE
Classical bust branding, fashion fragrance visual, fantasy character art, anatomy pin-up.

## 5. Bestiary boundary

`BESTIARY_BASE_B0` is a specialized entity/apparition branch and is **not** part of the default primary image language.

Do not introduce:
- angels;
- demons;
- hybrid creatures;
- multi-ocular entities;
- bestiary specimens

into ordinary R3/R4 production simply because they are canon-adjacent.

Bestiary content requires a later dedicated taxonomy/brief or an explicitly authorized application need.

Entity logic may influence ambiguity, classification and apparition behavior without importing literal creatures.

## 6. Family mixing rules

Default D2 composition:
- 1 dominant family;
- 1 supporting family or treatment overlay;
- optional 1 localized crimson event.

D3 may combine more, but every family must have a role.

Avoid “show all families” compositions.

Printed Evidence and Negative Shape may overlay any family where semantically justified.

## 7. Asset Cut List V1

Reject or quarantine:
- generic AI Gothic;
- skulls/statues/cathedrals as automatic dark mood;
- generic black marble / gold luxury;
- literal medical illustration as identity shorthand;
- cyan/neon medical or sci-fi visualization;
- nebula wallpaper;
- fantasy creature concept art outside B0;
- distressed/grunge overlays applied globally;
- blood/gore-first imagery;
- generic neural-network visuals;
- decorative contour maps and fake coordinates;
- botanical-vintage-pretty matter as central identity;
- any attractive asset whose rights state is unknown for intended production use.

## 8. Generation/source preflight

Before any future image call or sourcing:
1. name the family;
2. name the intended composition mode/application;
3. state the semantic role;
4. identify fixed Core/B1/R2 constraints;
5. state the variable being tested;
6. name the failure mode;
7. bind approved references/evidence explicitly;
8. assign initial rights/provenance state.

If those cannot be stated, do not generate/source the asset.
