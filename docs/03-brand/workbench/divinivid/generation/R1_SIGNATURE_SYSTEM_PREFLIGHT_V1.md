# DIVINIVID R1 Signature System Preflight V1

## Purpose

Define the five genuinely different R1 signature hypotheses required by the frozen Visual Identity Master Plan before any Figma write.

This document does not approve a wordmark.

It fixes:
- the question;
- the five hypotheses;
- what remains invariant;
- what each route is allowed to change;
- what counts as failure;
- the exact purpose of the first Figma call.

## R1 question

> Which signature architecture can make the word DIVINIVID distinctive, memorable and structurally native to the approved identity **without requiring historical imagery, texture, iconography or a hero composition**?

## Fixed variables

All five hypotheses must preserve:

- Brand name: `DIVINIVID`;
- P1 imposing dual-editorial personality;
- seriousness without extravagance;
- P2 Minimal / No-Icons;
- black/ivory monochrome as the first proof state;
- high legibility;
- contemporary usability;
- no occult/sigil shorthand;
- no literal Japanese symbols;
- no decorative anatomy;
- no generic luxury-serif treatment;
- no texture, imagery or crimson used to rescue weak lettering.

The first comparison tests **signature architecture only**.

## Five hypotheses

### S-A — Palindromic Axis

#### Core idea
Use the fact that `DIVINIVID` is an exact palindrome as structural information rather than a visual gimmick.

The wordmark is organized around the central `N` as a hinge/axis.

Left and right sequences must feel intentionally related:
`D I V I | N | I V I D`.

#### Allowed interventions
- controlled bilateral spacing;
- paired counterform logic;
- mirrored or corresponding cuts where readability survives;
- central-N emphasis by proportion or spacing;
- outer-D relationship;
- subtle shared construction across repeated I/V units.

#### What makes it distinct
The architecture is defined by the **whole word as a palindromic object**.

#### Hard fails
- mirror-text novelty;
- making half the word backwards;
- perfect symmetry that looks like a puzzle logo;
- illegibility;
- occult/bilateral sigil behavior;
- over-emphasizing the palindrome until DIVINIVID becomes a trick.

#### Learning target
Can the name itself provide enough structural signature to carry the brand without added symbol?

---

### S-B — Bilateral Body

#### Core idea
Translate the approved body/anatomy logic into letterform relationships, not anatomical illustration.

The signature behaves like a bilateral organism:
- paired mass;
- central spine;
- mirrored pressure;
- controlled asymmetry;
- related but non-identical counterforms.

#### Allowed interventions
- subtle left/right mass correspondence;
- a central vertical/spinal relationship;
- repeated paired terminals;
- tension between symmetry and living irregularity;
- negative-space behavior that suggests internal structure.

#### What makes it distinct
Unlike S-A, this route does **not** depend on palindrome mechanics as the primary concept.

Its logic is corporeal:
`BILATERAL ORDER -> CONTROLLED LIVING DEVIATION`.

#### Hard fails
- literal ribs, bones, veins or organs;
- biomorphic horror lettering;
- fantasy-metal typography;
- symmetry that merely duplicates S-A;
- decorative body references.

#### Learning target
Can DIVINIVID feel embodied and alive while remaining severe and editorial?

---

### S-C — Sacred Inscription

#### Core idea
Create authority through inscriptional monumentality rather than imagery.

The wordmark should feel:
- carved;
- declarative;
- ancient in weight but contemporary in execution;
- solemn;
- deliberately spaced.

Historical intelligence appears through proportion and presence, not period costume.

#### Allowed interventions
- inscriptional capital proportions;
- restrained serif/terminal emphasis;
- controlled optical irregularity;
- monumental tracking;
- one or two distinctive custom cuts;
- subtle vertical stress.

#### What makes it distinct
This is the least morphologically complex route.

Its signature comes from **authority, rhythm and proportion**.

#### Hard fails
- fashion/luxury wordmark;
- generic Didone;
- Roman-emperor cosplay;
- black + gold premium cliché;
- cathedral/gothic shorthand;
- excessive contrast that becomes fragile at small size.

#### Learning target
What is the minimum visual intervention required for DIVINIVID to feel inevitable rather than merely elegant?

---

### S-D — Mirrored Cut

#### Core idea
Use B1's active-negative-space and cut/reveal behavior directly inside the letterforms.

The signature is not mirrored as a whole.

Instead, a controlled family of cuts/counterforms creates:
- absence;
- reveal;
- correspondence;
- incomplete/complete relationships.

#### Allowed interventions
- repeated negative cuts;
- mirrored cut direction across selected letter pairs;
- voids that align across repeated letters;
- partial removal that preserves recognition;
- one consistent cut angle or geometry.

#### What makes it distinct
The identity is carried by **what is removed**, not by decorative additions.

This directly translates:
- negative shape;
- printed evidence;
- reveal;
- registration.

#### Hard fails
- stencil logo;
- random sliced-letter tech aesthetic;
- cyber/glitch cuts;
- excessive fragmentation;
- cuts without a repeatable construction rule.

#### Learning target
Can B1's strongest non-image mechanism become a compact wordmark signature?

---

### S-E — Custom Letterform Logic

#### Core idea
Use DIVINIVID's unusually small unique-glyph set as an advantage.

The word contains only four unique letters:
`D / I / V / N`.

Create a custom construction rule across those four glyphs so the signature is recognizable from:
- proportions;
- terminal behavior;
- stroke logic;
- counterform;
- repeated structural relation.

#### Allowed interventions
- custom D/I/V/N construction;
- one shared terminal/cut system;
- repeated proportion ratios;
- distinctive I behavior;
- controlled relation between V and N diagonals;
- a D counterform derived from the same rule.

#### What makes it distinct
This is the most system-oriented route.

Instead of designing one wordmark effect, it asks whether a **small native display alphabet logic** can emerge.

#### Hard fails
- over-designed custom type;
- novelty glyphs;
- unreadable V/N relationships;
- pseudo-runes;
- turning the project into a type-design exercise beyond the brand's needs.

#### Learning target
Can four custom glyph rules create the strongest long-term signature while remaining simple enough to reproduce?

# Comparison contract

## Constant proof set

Each S-A–S-E candidate must be shown in exactly the same proof conditions:

1. primary wordmark at large size;
2. black on ivory;
3. ivory on black;
4. 32px width-constrained check;
5. 16px/micro check where meaningful;
6. no imagery;
7. no texture;
8. no crimson;
9. no icon;
10. no ornamental frame.

Optional only after a route survives:
- reduced mark;
- monogram;
- favicon derivation.

Do not manufacture those before the architecture proves itself.

## Evaluation dimensions

Do not score for prettiness alone.

Evaluate:

1. **DIVINIVID specificity** — does it belong to this brand?
2. **P1 continuity** — does it preserve imposing editorial personality?
3. **readability** — immediate recognition of DIVINIVID;
4. **small-size survival**;
5. **monochrome strength**;
6. **distinctiveness without imagery**;
7. **system fertility** — can the logic extend to a reduced signature or later branded display logic?
8. **anti-genericity** — avoids dark-luxury/fashion/gothic/tech shorthand;
9. **construction clarity** — can another designer explain/reproduce the rule?
10. **restraint** — personality without extravagance.

## Gate semantics

- `PASS` — architecture deserves one targeted refinement;
- `MUTATE` — mechanism valid, specific correction needed;
- `KILL` — generic, gimmicky, unreadable or off-system.

The human owner may:
- select one;
- combine two only if the combination has a precise mechanism;
- reject all five and reopen R1 hypothesis design.

No automatic synthesis.

# First Figma-call contract

The first Figma call is **not yet authorized by this document**. It becomes authorized only after human approval of this preflight.

When authorized:

- `CALL_ID: R1-FIGMA-01-SIGNATURE-5UP`
- `ACTIVE_PHASE: R1_SIGNATURE_SYSTEM`
- `QUESTION: which of S-A–S-E best survives as a distinctive image-independent DIVINIVID signature?`
- `WHY_THIS_TOOL: exact geometry, consistent proof conditions, editable comparison, monochrome and small-size testing`
- `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH: Core + B1 + M1-B + P1 + P2 + R0 + this preflight`
- `VARIABLE_UNDER_TEST: signature architecture`
- `FIXED_VARIABLES: word, monochrome proof state, no imagery, no icon system, P1 personality, no new palette`
- `EXPECTED_OUTPUT: one native/editable five-up comparison sheet`
- `CALL_DOD: all five candidates shown under identical proof conditions; no clipping; no raster wordmark; no low-resolution proxy; no silent type substitution; visual inspection performed after write`
- `BUDGET: one comparison call + one targeted repair after human review`
- `FAILURE_ROUTE: stop and repair the construction method; do not reroll unrelated alternatives`
- `PROMOTION_RULE: EVIDENCE_PENDING until explicit human selection`

## Construction-method constraint

The Figma comparison must **not** pretend that a random available font is the approved production typeface.

Allowed methods:
1. native vector/custom construction derived from the approved P1 proportion/character; or
2. an explicitly labelled `CONSTRUCTION_SUBSTRATE / NOT PRODUCTION FONT` used only to test architecture, with custom interventions separated from the base typeface.

If a construction substrate is required, its selection is itself a bounded technical sub-step and cannot silently become R2 typography.
