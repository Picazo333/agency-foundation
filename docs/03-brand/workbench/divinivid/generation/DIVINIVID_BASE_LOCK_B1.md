# DIVINIVID Base Lock B1 — Route 3 + 4

## Decision
`BASE_LOCK_B1` is the principal expression/production base for the next DIVINIVID development phase.

It does **not** replace the Core. It is the currently preferred way to express the Core.

## Composition
B1 is the controlled synthesis of:

### Route 3 — Mokuhanga / printed evidence
Portable principles:
- layered registration;
- plate/impression logic;
- erosion and transfer;
- printed evidence;
- archival trace;
- tactile substrate;
- repetition with meaningful variation.

It does not require literal ukiyo-e motifs.

### Route 4 — Negative Shape / invisible body
Portable principles:
- figure revealed through absence;
- active negative space;
- ambiguous figure/ground;
- presence inferred from what is missing;
- material defining void;
- void defining material.

## Invariants inherited from Core
B1 preserves:
- Living Darkness;
- Living Crimson as semantic matter;
- ivory/bone evidence;
- aged-gold instrumentation;
- residual ultramar only;
- macro/micro duality;
- archival/editorial authority;
- ambiguous matter;
- dream depth;
- non-generic elegance.

## Japanese-influence rule
The influence remains structural and procedural.

Preferred:
- print process;
- negative space;
- asymmetry;
- layered registration;
- tactile evidence;
- controlled incompleteness.

Avoid by default:
- waves as automatic shorthand;
- kanji used decoratively;
- seals used as generic Japanese decoration;
- koi, dragons, masks or other motifs without semantic need;
- costume-like Japanization.

## Production behavior
Future work should preserve the feeling that:
- one material can produce multiple readings;
- one absence can organize multiple presences;
- the artifact is both image and evidence;
- printed layers reveal rather than merely decorate.

## Current role
B1 is the base for:
1. morphological development;
2. later brutalist elevation;
3. agency/editorial/web translation;
4. production-system exploration.

## Mutation rule
Every immediate mutation must change one major variable at a time.

No multi-axis reinvention.

## Reopen rule
B1 can be replaced only by explicit human approval after a directly comparable challenger demonstrates a material improvement.
