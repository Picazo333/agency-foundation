# DIVINIVID — 100 Moodboard Visual Universe Expansion Specification V1

**Program:** `DIVINIVID-VUE-100-V1`  
**Status:** PLAN LOCKED — human approved 2026-09-20  
**Execution:** 20 rounds × 5 independent 16:9 moodboards

## Governing rule

These 100 boards are **coverage contracts**, not invitations to rediscover the aesthetic. Every accepted board must add new system knowledge. Negative Canon is binding. No board counts merely because it is attractive.

Global invariants: near-absolute black + ivory + Living Crimson; aged gold for instrument/order; ultramar only selectively; anatomy by analogy; sacred beauty + disturbance; surgical information order; A/B/H intensity; motion as behavior. Cyan/neon, generic dark academia, generic occult, videogame fantasy, gore shortcuts, visible-grid modernism and five-in-one composites are prohibited.

### Saturation checkpoints

After boards 20, 40, 60 and 80: measure new-coverage yield, redundancy, family imbalance and remaining gaps. Remaining contracts may be reallocated only if the change increases coverage without reopening Canon.
