# DIVINIVID — Phase 5 Negative Evidence Ledger

Purpose: preserve failed-generation learning without allowing failed artifacts to contaminate future work.

## NE-01 — OA controlled mutations became too similar
Observed: multiple Oneiric Anatomy mutations shared nearly the same composition and visual solution despite different mutation labels.

Classification: `KILL AS COMPARISON EVIDENCE`.

Learning:
- slider deltas alone do not guarantee visible divergence;
- structural independence must be checked before human review;
- later prompts may not inherit generated composition by convenience.

Do not reuse:
- exact composition;
- exact figure pose;
- exact internal architecture;
- visual similarity as evidence that the route is stable.

May preserve only:
- the original OA-01 affinity signals explicitly named by the human owner.

---

## NE-02 — Safety-triggered corporeal generation failures
Observed: some corporeal/anatomical prompts were interpreted as graphical bodily harm.

Classification: `PROCESS FAILURE / NOT CREATIVE REJECTION`.

Learning:
- corporeal does not require injury;
- use atlas, skeletal, tendinous, membranous, botanical, radiographic, translucent, sectional and impossible-morphology language;
- safety rewrites must keep the experimental variable constant.

---

## NE-03 — Surreal-organic convergence produced a repeated creature family
Observed: the generator repeatedly selected a long-necked reptilian / winged / dragon-like solution.

Classification: `KILL AS POSITIVE EVIDENCE`.

Human feedback: repetition was explicitly rejected; same creature with small changes is not divergence.

Learning:
- “oneiric beast” is semantically underconstrained for generation;
- morphology family, silhouette, symmetry, appendage logic and posture must be specified or deliberately randomized as independent variables;
- fantasy-animal defaults are Anti-Canon unless explicitly selected later.

Prohibited future shortcut:
- using any of these dragon/wyvern artifacts as a reference image, parent image, style seed or composition seed.

---

## NE-04 — Identity-board round over-compressed the design problem
Observed: full generated boards attempted to invent organism, wordmark, typography, liturgical illustration, information architecture and application snippets in one image.

Classification: `KILL AS METHOD`.

Learning:
- a polished generated board can conceal weak identity reasoning;
- type rendered inside generated imagery is visual suggestion, not a typography system;
- image generation should produce ingredients; system assembly should be editable and deliberate;
- comparison boards need a fixed evidence contract.

---

## NE-05 — Liturgical signal became generic ornament
Observed: later boards repeated stars, moons, arches, floral borders and pseudo-manuscript devices without proving their conceptual role.

Classification: `MUTATE / DO NOT PROMOTE`.

Learning:
- liturgical does not mean “add medieval-looking decoration”;
- future liturgical tests must choose a mechanism: marginalia, illuminated initial, devotional framing, iconographic hierarchy, manuscript field logic, inscription behavior, or sacred-image composition;
- every liturgical device must have an information/compositional function.

---

## NE-06 — Premature application previews
Observed: web/application fragments appeared before signature, foundations and typography were established.

Classification: `METHOD DRIFT`.

Learning:
- Phase 5 may prove system fertility through controlled surface skeletons;
- it must not imply final web identity, font selection or application design;
- final application exploration follows downstream gates in the frozen master plan.

---

## NE-07 — Unequal evidence across comparison boards
Observed: some artifacts were hero-heavy, others dossier-heavy, others included pseudo-brand applications.

Classification: `INVALID COMPARISON SET`.

Learning:
- every batch declares a common output type, information burden, aspect ratio and polish level;
- if one candidate receives extra applications or richer typography, the comparison must be rerun or treated as exploratory only.

---

# Positive evidence that survives these failures

Negative evidence does not erase earlier human pull.

Retained signals:
- OA-01: solemn oneiric threshold, crimson semantic interior, strong original pull;
- BS-01: impossible organism + serious classification/editorial containment;
- BS-04: strongest later signal — surreal dislocation with organic materiality;
- Dark Revelation: desired elegance through concealment/revelation;
- liturgical/manuscript illustration: desired but currently under-proven.

These are principles, not pixel references.

# Enforcement

Before any future visual generation:
1. check this ledger;
2. name which negative-evidence risks are active;
3. declare the prevention rule;
4. reject the batch before human review if it repeats a documented failure mechanically.
