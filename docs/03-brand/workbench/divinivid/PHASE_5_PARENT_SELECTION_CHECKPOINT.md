## Interpretation rule
`PARENT` means the complete route remains an active candidate identity lineage.

`DONOR` means only a named mechanism may be borrowed in a controlled mutation. The donor route is not silently blended into the parent.

`HOLD` means the route remains approved evidence and may return later if a specific unresolved need justifies it.
