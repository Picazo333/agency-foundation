# ADR-0006 — Diagnostic-led vertical productized studio as the working thesis

Status: **PROPOSED**
Date: 2026-09-16
Proposed by: Claude/CoWork Agency Master Plan workstream (Issue #2)
Decided by: human project owner

## Context

`PROJECT_STATE.md` lists "agency archetype/business model" as an open decision. The Claude/CoWork
workstream was asked to reconcile the evidence and determine which archetypes deserve further
consideration, treating BOLD's anti-generic-AI-agency conclusion as a falsification input rather
than a predetermined answer (`ADR-0004`, `BOLD_CONTRARIAN_SUMMARY.md`).

Nine archetypes were compared in `docs/02-strategy/thesis/STRATEGY_RECONCILIATION.md` using
elimination logic against four disqualifiers, rather than weighted numeric scoring — because
weighting fourteen ungraded criteria would produce a decisive number from undecidable inputs, which
is precisely the false precision `E-04` prohibits.

## Decision

Adopt **M8 — a diagnostic-led, vertical, productized studio** as the **working thesis under
validation**:

> A paid diagnostic measures where a specific kind of business loses revenue between demand
> arriving and demand being converted and served. Bounded, fixed-price productized builds close the
> measured gaps. An operated recurring layer keeps them closed and keeps measuring. AI and
> automation are the mechanism that makes this viable for one person; they are never the pitch.

**This is not a freeze.** Approving this ADR authorises ninety days of validation effort and
activates the stop rule below. It does not make the model canon, does not select a vertical, and
does not commit to any price.

## Alternatives considered

| Archetype | Outcome | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Classic multidisciplinary agency | Killed | Portfolio-gated with no portfolio; commoditised axis |
| Horizontal AI/automation agency | Killed as a category; capability retained as a mechanism | Names the supplier's tools, not the buyer's problem; commoditising (`E-17`) |
| Productized boutique | Survives — absorbed into M8 | Answers *what to sell*, not *who to tell* |
| Consultancy-led | Killed as a model; the diagnostic instrument retained | Credential-gated; produces nothing countable inside an engagement |
| Creative-technology / systems studio | **Demoted to future destination** | Best fit for the founder's taste (`E-07`); portfolio-gated today. Reachable in 18–36 months on proof produced by another model |
| Vertical specialist | Survives — absorbed into M8 | Strongest answer to acquisition difficulty |
| White-label / subcontract | Killed; named as a trap | Structurally commoditised; forecloses every other model |
| Productized horizontal, marketed vertically | Killed as a strategy; retained as a 90-day hedging tactic | Never accumulates a domain asset |

## Why

1. **It is the only candidate where proof is a byproduct of the entry offer.** The diagnostic
   captures a baseline in week one, so every engagement is a case study by construction. For a
   supplier whose binding constraint is the absence of references (`A-06`), that property is worth
   more than any other.
2. **It resolves the BOLD signal correctly.** AI is killed as a category and kept as a mechanism,
   which preserves the founder's real advantage without adopting a commoditising identity.
3. **The vertical component answers the binding constraint.** `E-23` identifies buyer access, not
   plan quality, as the scarcest resource; a vertical makes target lists enumerable and referral
   loops dense.
4. **Productization fits solo capacity.** It converts founder attention into a repeatable shape and
   is the precondition for later delegation.

## Consequences

- Ninety days of founder capacity (55–70%, `FIELD_VALIDATION_PLAN.md` §9) is committed to validation
  rather than to billable work. `U-03` (runway) must support this.
- Generic agency, AI-agency and white-label revenue paths are deliberately forfeited
  (`AGENCY_THESIS.md` §10, `RF-1`–`RF-9`).
- The craft-led model the founder's taste favours is explicitly deferred, with a stated reopen
  condition.
- **Stop rule activates:** after this plan merges, no further strategic planning artifact may be
  created until **10 qualified buyer conversations** are logged in `docs/06-validation/interviews/`.
  Delivery templates, sales instruments and validation tooling are permitted.

## Evidence / assumptions

- Supporting: `E-17`, `E-18` (`SIGNAL`); `E-12`, `E-21` (founder capability); `E-23`, `E-25`
  (`INFERENCE`).
- Load-bearing and **unvalidated**: `P-1`–`P-4` in `STRATEGY_RECONCILIATION.md` §5; `A-07`, `A-08`,
  `A-11`.
- Every price in the supporting model is an `INFERRED RANGE` (`E-22`). No `PUBLIC PRICE` or
  `PRICE SIGNAL` record exists.
- Acknowledged in `RED_TEAM_REVIEW.md`: there is no evidence the founder can sell (RT-02); the
  problem may be supplier-invented (RT-04); the opportunity cost may exceed the expected value
  (RT-09).

## Reopen if

- `DECISION_TREE.md` D5 path C: zero monetisation from 20 qualified conversations.
- D4 resonance fails at ≤1 of 10 after one reframe.
- D3 access fails after two segment rotations.
- Validation shows inbound demand for distinctive craft exceeding outbound demand for operational
  repair — which would favour M5 and invert the plan.
- `U-01`/H-04 reveal achievable price levels that make the economics unviable at solo scale.

Any of these requires a superseding ADR naming the selected pivot from `DECISION_TREE.md` D7,
written **within 7 days** of the gate failing.
