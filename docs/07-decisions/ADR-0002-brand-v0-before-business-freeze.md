# ADR-0002 — Brand V0 before business freeze

Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-09-15

## Decision
Allow naming and preliminary visual identity work now, but do not freeze Brand V1 until a Brand/Business Fit Review.

## Why
There is enough visual signal to progress, while final ICP/offer/positioning remain open.

## Consequences
Creative exploration can continue without forcing the commercial model to fit the aesthetic.

## Reopen if
The emerging business model fundamentally conflicts with Brand V0.
