# ADR-0010 — AI is a delivery mechanism, never the category

Status: **PROPOSED**
Date: 2026-09-16
Proposed by: Claude/CoWork Agency Master Plan workstream (Issue #2)
Decided by: human project owner

## Context

`RISK_REGISTER.md` lists "Generic AI-agency positioning" as a Medium/High risk mitigated by strategy
reconciliation and the BOLD contrarian input. `BOLD_CONTRARIAN_SUMMARY.md` holds that a generic
horizontal AI/automation agency may be commercially weak and commoditised, while explicitly not
being a canonical decision.

The founder's strongest demonstrated capability is multi-agent AI orchestration (`E-21`), which
creates a real temptation to lead with it.

## Decision

**Kill AI as a category. Keep AI as a mechanism.**

| Context | AI's place |
|---|---|
| Category name, tagline, first sentence, cold outreach | **Absent** |
| Website hero | **Absent** |
| Proposal "how we work" | Present, factual, one paragraph |
| Delivery conversations | Present — how speed and price are achievable |
| Case studies | Present only where it changed the outcome |
| Pricing justification | **Never** — a price is never explained by naming the tools |

Positive form when asked directly: *"Yes, heavily. It's how one team delivers in four weeks what
normally takes twelve. It's how we do it, not what you're buying."*

**Disclosure obligation:** where AI systems process client or customer data, this is disclosed in
the proposal and covered contractually. Never denied (`EC-9`, `AI-6`). This obligation overrides any
positioning convenience.

**AI feature restraint on owned surfaces:** the agency should be slow to add AI features to its own
website. An AI chat widget on the homepage of a business that refuses to lead with AI would
contradict the positioning in the most visible possible place.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Rejected because |
|---|---|
| Lead with AI | `E-17`; names the supplier's tools rather than the buyer's problem; buyers hold marketing/ops/IT budget lines, not an "AI" line; commoditising |
| Hide AI entirely | Dishonest; breaks `EC-9`; and unnecessary — the capability is a legitimate answer to "how can one person do this?" |
| AI as a secondary brand pillar | Still anchors the category; still invites comparison with AI shops |

## Why

The scarce input has moved from capability to diagnosis. When implementation capability becomes
universal, the defensible position is not "we have AI" but "we know precisely what to point it at."
Positioning on the capability positions on the commodity.

There is also a direct conflict with the founder's own documented visual anti-profile, which
explicitly rejects "purple/cyan AI gradients" and "neon tech" (`E-07`). Leading with AI would
require a visual language the founder has already rejected on taste grounds — the commercial
argument and the aesthetic one point the same way.

## Consequences

- Forfeits novelty-seeking inbound and buyers with a dedicated AI budget. Accepted deliberately.
- Requires discipline when a prospect is visibly excited by AI: the correct move is to redirect to
  the outcome, not to ride the enthusiasm.
- Brand V1 must not signal "AI company" (`BR-8`, `BC-08`).
- The AI governance rules `AI-1`–`AI-8` (`DELIVERY_OS.md` §7.1) bind regardless of positioning:
  no client personal data without a contractual basis, no clinical records ever, every AI-produced
  figure verified against source.

## Evidence / assumptions

`E-17`, `E-18` (`SIGNAL`); `E-07` (`FACT`, anti-profile); `E-21` (`SIGNAL`, capability).
`RED_TEAM_REVIEW.md` RT-06: the capability advantage is the fastest-depreciating asset in the plan,
which strengthens rather than weakens this decision — an identity built on a depreciating asset
depreciates with it.

## Reopen if

- H-11 or field evidence shows that a specific segment pays a **premium** for explicitly AI-labelled
  work at a level that outweighs the commoditisation risk. In that case this ADR is revisited by a
  superseding ADR, not quietly ignored.
- A durable, defensible AI capability emerges that competitors cannot replicate — at which point it
  may be a product, which is a different decision from a category.
