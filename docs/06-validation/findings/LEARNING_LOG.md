# Learning Log

> Weekly record of what field contact actually taught. Format defined in
> `../FIELD_VALIDATION_PLAN.md` §7. One entry per week, from the week validation begins.
>
> **Entries are produced by the weekly review procedure:**
> [`../field-kit/14_WEEKLY_VALIDATION_REVIEW.md`](../field-kit/14_WEEKLY_VALIDATION_REVIEW.md)
> (20–30 min, same day each week). Two consecutive blank *"what I was wrong about"* entries trigger
> the escalation in that file's §8.

## Rules

1. **"What I was wrong about" may not be empty for two consecutive weeks.** An empty entry means
   either no learning is happening, or it is not being admitted.
2. Record what buyers said, in their words. Not what it seemed to mean.
3. Record what did *not* come up. An expected pain that never surfaces is evidence.
4. Update `../hypotheses/HYPOTHESIS_REGISTER.md` in the same sitting.
5. Entries are append-only. Do not revise an earlier week to look more prescient.

```markdown
**Conversations held:** n (segment breakdown)
**Hypotheses touched:** H-xx, H-yy

**What I learned**
-

**What surprised me**
-

-

**Buyer phrases logged (verbatim)**
-

**Assumption updates**
- A-xx: was …, now …, on the basis of … (grade: SIGNAL/FACT)

**Next week's single focus**
-
```
