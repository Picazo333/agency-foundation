# 05 — Teardown Template

> Copy the block in §1 for each teardown. Produce it in plain type — no brand system required
> (`BRAND_BUSINESS_INTERFACE.md` §6: Brand V1 is **not** a prerequisite for validation).
> Keep it to **one page**. Everything longer reads as an audit and triggers defensiveness.

## 1. The template

```markdown
# [Business name] — what's visible from outside
Prepared by [name] · [date] · 5-minute video: [link]

## What this is
I spent about twenty minutes looking at your public booking and enquiry path, and I sent one
general enquiry on [date] to time the response. This is what I could see. I can't see your booking
data, your enquiry volume, or what any of this actually costs you — so I haven't guessed. Where
I've drawn a conclusion, I've marked it as mine and it may be wrong.

## What I observed

**1. [Short factual heading]**
[Exactly what happened, with numbers and timestamps.]
*My reading (may be wrong):* [one sentence, clearly labelled]

**2. [Short factual heading]**
[Observable.]
*My reading (may be wrong):* [one sentence]

**3. [Short factual heading]**
[Observable.]
*My reading (may be wrong):* [one sentence]

## What I could NOT tell from outside
- [e.g. whether that enquiry was typical or an unusually busy day]
- [e.g. how many enquiries you get, and through which channel]
- [e.g. whether any of this affects your bookings at all]

## Two questions I'd genuinely like to ask
1. [Open question from the research record]
2. [Open question]

If you've got 25 minutes, I'm mapping how [segment] groups your size handle this. Happy to send
the video either way — no obligation, and nothing to buy.

[name] · [contact]
```

## 2. Worked structure — SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE, NOT EVIDENCE

> **SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — NOT EVIDENCE.** No real business is described. No observation below was
> made. This exists solely to show the *shape* of a compliant teardown. It must never be sent,
> published, counted or cited.

```markdown
<!-- SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — NOT EVIDENCE — DELETE BEFORE USE -->
# [SYNTHETIC] Example Clinic Group — what's visible from outside

## What I observed

**1. The web form and the WhatsApp line behave differently**
[SYNTHETIC] I sent the same question through both on Tuesday at 14:02. WhatsApp replied in 11
minutes. The web form was answered Thursday at 11:40 — 45.6 hours.
*My reading (may be wrong):* the two channels may not share a queue.

**2. Two of your three location pages don't show the booking widget**
[SYNTHETIC] It appears on [Site A] but not on [Site B] or [Site C], where the only option is a
phone number.
*My reading (may be wrong):* the pages may have been built at different times.

**3. No follow-up after my enquiry went quiet**
[SYNTHETIC] I didn't reply to the Thursday message. Nothing further arrived in the next 72 hours.
*My reading (may be wrong):* there may be no automatic nudge for unanswered enquiries.

## What I could NOT tell from outside
- Whether Tuesday was a typical day
- How many enquiries arrive through each channel
- Whether any of this changes your booked appointments
```

Note what the example does **not** contain: no percentage, no revenue figure, no claim about staff,
no benchmark, no mention of anything for sale.

## 3. Pre-send checklist

- [ ] Three observations, each reproducible by a stranger today
- [ ] Every inference labelled "my reading (may be wrong)"
- [ ] "What I could NOT tell" section present and honest
- [ ] Test enquiry disclosed with its date
- [ ] No revenue, conversion, or cost claim anywhere (`T-1`, `T-2`, `T-8`)
- [ ] No benchmark or "typically…" statistic (`T-6`)
- [ ] No mention of the incumbent supplier (`T-7`)
- [ ] **No pitch, no price, no description of the diagnostic** (`T-11`, protects H-10)
- [ ] Ends with questions, not a CTA
- [ ] One page
- [ ] Total time ≤ 3 hours
- [ ] Logged in `02_TARGET_ACCOUNT_LEDGER.md`
