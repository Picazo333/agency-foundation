## Local authority
Authority: **documentation and operationalization** after Brand V1 decisions exist. It may turn approved identity/voice decisions into specific, testable rules and do/don't examples.

It may NOT:
- invent missing mission, values, voice, palette or identity choices;
- promote provisional work to Brand V1;
- replace specialist identity/accessibility/rights specs;
- dilute failure thresholds into vague adjectives.

## DIVINIVID use
Activate only after Comparative Brand Direction Review and Brand/Business Fit Review select a route. Guidelines must preserve the central tension system while making high-frequency decisions repeatable for designers, writers, agents and vendors.

## Output contract
Brand foundation; personality is/is-not definitions; voice/tone mechanics where approved; logo/color/type/imagery/motion usage; concrete do/don't examples; edge cases; links to specialist specs.
