## Local authority
Authority: **curation and consistency review** for reference sets and generated asset families. It may define light key, palette/temperature, tone, crop, subject distance, texture and set-level consistency; maintain final picks and a cut list; and recommend restrained grading.

It may NOT:
- search for or license assets by itself;
- override the active route grammar;
- rescue an off-brief image merely because it is individually strong;
- promote staging assets to approved production status.

## DIVINIVID use
Use in Reference Atlas and Asset Proof Kit. Judge sets together. Preserve deliberate tension between divine/grotesque and light/dark without flattening all material into one filter. A coherent set may contain contrast when that contrast is explicitly part of the grammar.

## Output contract
Visual-system spec; final picks mapped to intended placement; cut list with one-line reasons; treatment/grade notes; explicit drift findings.
