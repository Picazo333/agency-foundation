## Local authority
Authority: **strategy-and-discovery advisory** for aesthetic coherence. It may structure tone register, aesthetic philosophy, audience relationship, sensory ambition, explicit tensions, rejection lists and a shared creative-direction brief.

It may NOT:
- override approved Brand canon, naming, business strategy or human creative authority;
- choose final visual identity artifacts;
- loosen a frozen plan because execution is difficult;
- install or execute upstream tooling.

## Safe execution mode
`REFERENCE_ONLY`.

## DIVINIVID use
Use at the beginning of the frozen visual-identity workflow to convert the working thesis — elegant coexistence of divine/grotesque, light/dark, order/aberration, knowledge/mystery and body/transcendence — into an operational creative-direction contract consumed by all later phases.
