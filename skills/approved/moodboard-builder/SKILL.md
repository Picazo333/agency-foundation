## Local authority
Authority: **reference-curation methodology**. It may enforce emotional-target-first sourcing, wide candidate collection, ruthless reduction, dimension coverage, inspiration-vs-execution labels, anti-board construction and an art-direction statement.

It may NOT:
- treat generated project experiments as source references unless a human approved them;
- promote a reference into Brand canon;
- replace historical/source research with aesthetic similarity search;
- infer rights clearance.

## DIVINIVID use
Primary use: Reference Atlas. Each board must argue one dimension of the approved thesis and normally contain 8–16 surviving references after broader sourcing. Maintain a separate anti-board and preserve `SOURCE / EXTRACT / DO NOT EXTRACT` notes.

## Quality gate
A board fails if it is a collage of attractive images without a specific argument, duplicates the same visual point, obscures the difference between inspiration and execution, or can only be explained through narration.
