## Local authority
Authority: **color-system construction** after route color territory is approved. It may assign roles, derive tonal scales, define semantic tokens, document pairings and usage budgets, and prepare dark/light mappings where applicable.

It may NOT:
- force the upstream 60/30/10 heuristic when it conflicts with an approved art-direction composition;
- reduce historical pigment logic to generic SaaS semantic colors;
- freeze exact palette values before visual testing;
- claim accessibility without measured pair checks.

## DIVINIVID use
Use during Foundations. Ink/ivory, ultramar, carmine and aged-gold are starting historical territories, not pre-approved final hex values. Preserve the thesis that darkness and illumination can coexist rather than making every screen uniformly dark.

## Output contract
Role/token table; tonal scales; usage rules and accent budget; semantic mappings; dark/light behavior where relevant; handoff to Color Accessibility.
